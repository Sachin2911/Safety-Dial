# Evolutionary improvement on ManiGuard

**Current research direction, updated 19 October 2026.**

**The strongest next research bet is a reusable, bounded controller that evolves how a frozen ManiGuard VLA samples its actions.** Target one shared observation- and instruction-conditioned controller across the suite's released pi0.5 family-specific frozen policies, using the policy's native flow-noise input and actual simulator outcomes. Jar should be the first physical scientific target; Clutter is the predeclared capacity fallback. Neither choice restricts the intended architecture to that family. This is a recommendation, not an experimentally discovered winner. The proposed contribution is budgeted evolutionary adaptation for physical safe completion, with a tested explanation of when evolutionary reuse and evaluation allocation help. Noise steering, frozen-policy adaptation and evolutionary VLA improvement already have close precedents. A convincing result therefore needs to beat the contemporaneous native checkpoint, a strong fixed correction, matched random search and appropriate ordinary learning, with independent evaluation and complete interaction accounting.

Research reviewed on **19 October 2026**. This document is the current research direction, with a prospective method and evaluation design; it launches no experiment and authorizes no rental. The [earlier Phase 3 plans](docs/research/history/20261019/README.md) and frozen protocols remain preserved as historical evidence. The [agent execution prompt](docs/research/research_notes/ManiGuard%20latent%20evolution%20goal%20prompt.md) supplies the current allocation and GitHub/Hugging Face handoff. The avenue comparison and staged evaluation are recorded below.

## Agent execution contract: diagnose, repair and continue

**When assigned implementation and execution, persist through technical failures and disappointing development results until the authorized study is complete or a genuine external or resource limit prevents further useful work. A failed check creates a recovery task; it does not complete the assignment.** Implement, diagnose, repair, validate and resume independently within the agreed scope. Routine reversible fixes already covered by that scope need no fresh confirmation. A diagnosis-only report, the first unsuccessful repair or an early poor score is insufficient grounds to hand the task back. The objective is a functioning, fairly evaluated method and reproducible evidence, including supported negative results.

### Prepare execution that can recover

Before launching, freeze a versioned protocol and stage schedule covering development, comparative search, independent evaluation and recovery allowances. Record the task/family partitions, controller and feature definitions, bounds, objective, baselines, seeds, validity rules, interaction limits and time allocation. Reserve diagnosis and repair capacity within the existing allowance while protecting the complete comparison and final audit. The illustrative eight-hour/64-attempt pilot remains its own ceiling; later extensions and cross-family work need their separately scoped allocation.

Maintain a durable status and recovery log in the current study's run directory. It must identify the active stage, code/configuration revisions, last completed receipt, remaining work, consumed and remaining budgets, current failure hypothesis and next executable action. Save optimizer state, random-generator states, candidate ancestry and a completed/pending evaluation ledger at safe boundaries. Use new output paths and preserve raw evidence. After an interruption, reconcile saved state with completed receipts before resuming; an interrupted episode remains charged and needs an explicit status. Do not assume simulator state can be restored exactly. A context boundary or process restart should lead to continuation from this record.

### Apply the recovery loop whenever something goes wrong

1. **Capture the failure.** Preserve the command, exit status, logs, affected observations/actions, seeds, model and configuration identifiers, and all elapsed time, policy queries and simulator interactions. Mark affected records explicitly so they cannot silently become successful or missing trials.
2. **Find the cause.** Reproduce the smallest informative case. Distinguish environment/interface defects, inactive controls, evaluation variability, insufficient task competence, ineffective search and a valid negative scientific result. Write a concrete hypothesis and a check that could disprove it. A task timeout alone does not identify the cause.
3. **Repair and verify.** Apply the smallest justified change, run a targeted regression or integration check, and establish that the intended mechanism now operates. If a repair fails, update the hypothesis and use a different informative check. Repeating the same unsuccessful command without new evidence is not progress.
4. **Resume the interrupted stage.** Recover the work queue, preserve completed valid results and finish the affected comparison under consistent conditions. Infrastructure replacements follow the frozen validity rules and finite replacement allowance; a valid poor outcome remains in the record.
5. **Improve through declared development iterations.** When the pipeline works but the method underperforms, inspect the development evidence and test a specific explanation within the reserved allowance. Version any changed representation, feature set, bounds or search procedure, and freeze it before its comparative evaluation. Complete an already valid frozen comparison even when EA trails its competitors. A subsequent method revision is a separately identified cohort with prospectively matched comparisons and fully charged development costs.

Use these concrete diagnoses rather than treating an unsuccessful stage as a dead end:

- **Steering has little or no effect:** trace the explicit noise input through the server, sampler, transforms and executed action prefix. Check shapes, channel mapping, bounds, saturation and whether parent mutations actually change actions. Use paired fixed-input probes. If bounded native steering is genuinely ineffective, carry the evidence into the designated direct-residual fallback under its own protocol and available allocation.
- **A trigger never fires or a proposed mechanism never activates:** inspect eligibility and activation counts, units, thresholds and causal inputs on development cases. Repair and demonstrate activation before spending on its optimizer comparison. An inactive mechanism has not received a meaningful test.
- **Nominally repeated rollouts disagree:** compare resets, initial images, preprocessing, seeds, noise and first proposals before attributing differences to the controller. Correct identifiable defects and use declared replication for remaining stochasticity. Preserve the variability in the analysis.
- **Task completion stays low or evolution does not improve:** inspect executed trajectories, stage progress, candidate diversity, mutation effects and selection noise. Check whether failures indicate missing task competence, insufficient controller authority or poor search. Use justified development tests of bias/scale, persistence or response-informed coordinates within their finite allowance. A larger search budget is not a diagnosis.
- **An optional predictor or representation fails its audit:** repair its measurable failure within the development allowance or continue an applicable direct-outcome route that does not depend on it. Record what remains untested. Activation gating and other distinct avenues retain their own integration and allocation requirements.

### Preserve the scientific comparison while continuing

Treat valid timeouts, violations and unfinished episodes as outcomes. Preserve unsuccessful candidates and account for every attempted repair, probe and trial. Keep all baseline information, tuning and interaction allowances matched. An implementation defect discovered during comparison requires an explicit affected-record audit and a versioned, comparable repair plan. Preserve the original records and charge reruns; replacement and resource ceilings still apply.

Use development cases for method changes. Keep the independent audit and held-out-family outcomes reserved for their declared evaluation. Once inspected, those cases are exposed and cannot be reused as untouched confirmation of a revised method. A concrete implementation bug found during audit may require fresh confirmation data within a prospectively available allowance. Preserve both the original audit and the repair history. An unfavorable audit score alone is no reason to tune against it or rerun until favorable.

### Hand back a completed study or a resumable blocker

Continue through all authorized stages and applicable recovery paths. End execution when the scoped study and required analysis are complete, the user explicitly stops it, or a documented hard budget, authorization or external dependency prevents further useful authorized work. At a resource boundary, preserve state and release compute according to the agreed shutdown policy, then complete feasible local diagnosis, code repair and analysis. If a scientific route exhausts its declared checks and fallbacks, produce the supported result and complete the remaining reporting work.

Before handing back an unresolved blocker, finish independent work and provide the exact blocking evidence, attempted repairs and their outcomes, remaining budget, restart command and state location, and the smallest external change needed to resume. Every handoff must state which implementation, physical comparisons and transfer tests actually completed, which remain untested, and whether the next step is technical repair, further development or a new allocation. Continue toward a defensible answer; a positive result for evolution is not a condition of completion.

## Past experiments identify the missing behavioural lever

UNISafe establishes the distinction we must preserve. On 64 reserved starts per controller, completion, physical-or-boundary violation and unfinished counts were **2/6/56 for native**, **55/7/2 for fixed hold**, **56/6/2 for random search**, and **54/8/2 for both policy gradient and evolution**. Evolution's large completion improvement was real, but a simple rule explained it; improved completion did not establish improved safety. The causal diagnosis found the actor opening before the first safety replacement, and the repair largely survived removal of filtering. Our useful lesson is to keep a strong fixed comparator and diagnose what the adapter changes before assigning credit to the optimizer. ([Completed UNISafe study](docs/research/reports/UNISafe%20grasp%20maintenance%20and%20evolutionary%20adaptation.md), [causal diagnosis](docs/research/reports/UNISafe%20release%20and%20switching%20diagnosis.md)).

The six-hypothesis ManiGuard study completed **196 full engineering episodes**, covering replanning, motion moderation, prerequisite guarding, grasp persistence, retreat/retry and return after intervention. It was not a trained optimizer comparison. Retreat moved the robot as requested without repairing the task; additional replanning and delayed release activated without repeatable safe-completion gains. A privileged Jar guard recorded safe completion, violating and unfinished counts of 4/2/8 against identity's 2/4/9, each across 12 trials; unfinished episodes can also violate. The originally promising case did not reproduce. Correcting joint-limit reconstruction removed a genuine interface error without establishing a successful repair. Some benchmark-labelled Jar completions also lacked the requested closure prerequisite. These results separate command authority, task competence, safety and goal semantics. ([Six-hypothesis study](docs/research/reports/ManiGuard%20six-hypothesis%20study.md), [Jar repeats](docs/research/reports/ManiGuard%20Jar%20guard%20and%20motion%20repeats.md), [controller-limit correction](docs/research/reports/ManiGuard%20controller-limit%20guard%20comparison.md)).

Phase two implemented a richer recurrent controller and EA, random and PPO paths, but its comparative experiment remains incomplete. Nine of 22 paired observations disagreed despite shared initial genomes, nominal cases and seeds. Initial camera images and first proposals differed before intervention. Jar produced two safe completions among 22 inspected partial episodes, while Stack frequently never grasped. Those observations expose evaluation uncertainty and a possible competence floor; they do not demonstrate an evolutionary advantage or prove rendering variation is the sole cause. The separate 192-episode replicate diagnostic remains a protocol whose completed receipts need locating, not a completed result in this review. ([Active evidence record](docs/research/history/20261019/phase3.md), [phase-two development](docs/research/research_notes/ManiGuard%20phase2%20development.md)).

Part 1 did complete evolution. Its selected controller achieved **4/8 safe completions, 2/8 violating episodes and 2/8 unfinished episodes**, against identity's **5/8, 0/8 and 3/8**. No matched random or ordinary-learning comparison was included. Part 2's adaptive trigger never queried early, so its intended mechanism was untested. Part 3's forecast audit failed before the physical optimizer comparison; Part 4's warning model alarmed on 2,045/2,229 negative development windows before acquisition began. Neither unlaunched comparison supplies evidence against its optimizer. The synthetic demonstration supplies no empirical evidence. ([Part 1](docs/research/reports/ManiGuard%20evolutionary%20safety%20dial%20pilot.md), [Part 2](docs/research/reports/ManiGuard%20Part2%20correction%20triggered%20feedback.md), [Part 3](docs/research/reports/ManiGuard%20Part3%20model%20assisted%20evolution.md), [Part 4](docs/research/reports/ManiGuard%20Part4%20counterexample%20premise.md)).

The latest saved-trace diagnostic further weakens a cap/timing-only research bet. A uniform .04 rad cap changes **55/18,657 factual controls**, and the new feedback trigger has **3/482 eligible diagnostic prefixes**, all on one task. Direction-preserving scaling corrects command geometry on those saved inputs, but these are counterfactual commands evaluated at factual states. They establish neither a changed future trajectory nor physical benefit. The earlier Push-T and Walker work likewise argues for separating measured dynamics from prediction and readout errors, rather than adding another learned evaluator before direct adaptation works. ([Recovery diagnostic](docs/research/research_notes/ManiGuard%20recovery%20checkin%20diagnostic.json), [preserved model-study evidence](archive/legacy/docs/mainPlan/reports/through-s4-20260927/report.md)).

## Native steering leads eight avenues with different prerequisites

**First priority is shared native noise steering.** It changes action generation rather than only limiting an already chosen target. General applicability is an architectural requirement: no Jar-specific closure genes, scene IDs or family-specific deployed adapters in the target claim. Pinned openpi exposes `Policy.infer(obs, noise=...)`, and its flow sampler accepts explicit initial noise. A compact controller could therefore influence approach, relative joint motion or contact behaviour while retaining the frozen VLA's camera and instruction processing. This is source-supported feasibility, not demonstrated steerability of our checkpoints. The current websocket path does not forward noise, and the pinned ManiGuard files must be checked on the actual host. ([Pinned policy API](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Physical-Intelligence/openpi/215abfb217dbac7d5f1273282331b9b1866c0479/src/openpi/policies/policy.py), [pinned sampler](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Physical-Intelligence/openpi/215abfb217dbac7d5f1273282331b9b1866c0479/src/openpi/models/pi0.py), [current server](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NU-IDEAS-Lab/ManiGuard/main/maniguard/serve/openpi_native.py)).

**Second is a bounded action residual, the single representation fallback.** Adding a small direction-changing correction provides authority that a cap lacks, without requiring another policy query. Start with native joint targets; a Cartesian variant additionally needs validated kinematics, frames and joint-limit handling. Residual learning already improves frozen chunked policies in ResiP and ResFiT, making ordinary residual RL a serious comparator. Its common physical joint interface could transfer better if separately fine-tuned policies have incompatible latent directions. Reject it if corrections disrupt coordination or the relevant decision requires unavailable semantic information. ([ResiP](https://residual-assembly.github.io/), [ResFiT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.19301)).

**Third is a guarded task program or subgoal schedule.** Jar's close-then-carry structure makes it attractive, and evolution can search discrete sequences. However, accepting a text prompt does not prove the frozen checkpoint can execute a reliable subskill. Switching also needs observable guards. The official prompt study fine-tunes separate representation-specific models, which does not establish inference-only prompt steering. Genetic-programmed behaviour trees and InterEvolve further narrow structural novelty. Test component skills and nonprivileged guards before spending on program search. ([ManiGuard paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.17386v1), [GP behaviour trees](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.03252), [InterEvolve](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02196)).

**Fourth, noise-aware evaluation allocation, belongs inside a working adaptation route.** Repeated elites and finite racing can reduce promotion of lucky candidates, but consume search depth. PSyCo, noise-resilient ES and uncertain quality-diversity methods already address related problems. Its value here must be demonstrated against fixed replication and equally funded adaptive allocation for competitors. It cannot rescue a controller that lacks useful behaviour. **Fifth, reuse of the existing recurrent cap/query/delay controller, is an inexpensive reference.** That interface already contains state-dependent execution modes; renaming them task phases adds no new authority. ([PSyCo](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scico.2021.102620), [noise-resilient ES](https://www.cmap.polytechnique.fr/~nikolaus.hansen/proceedings/2019/GECCO/proceedings/proceedings_files/pap144s3-file1.pdf), [existing controller](experiments/helpers/maniguard_phase2_adapter.py)).

**Sixth is a QD repertoire of steering controllers.** Complementary approaches could matter across task conditions, but the method needs repeatable descriptors and a deployable selector. An oracle choosing the best controller after seeing outcomes is an upper bound. Extract-QD and QDAC supply close precedents and essential comparisons. **Seventh is evolutionary scene acquisition followed by repair.** It can contribute only if valid acquired scenarios improve a fixed learner on fresh tasks. Existing QD scenario generation, ACCEL and PLR make failure yield alone insufficient. Our current warning premise adds an unresolved dependency. ([Extract-QD](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.06585v1), [QDAC](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/grillotti24a.html), [QD scenarios](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.04283), [ACCEL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.01302), [PLR](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/jiang21b.html)).

**Eighth is online evolutionary action search using a learned scorer or world model.** It offers substantial action authority but requires trustworthy prospective ranking and repeated deployment queries. VLA-Pilot already evolves frozen-VLA action candidates using a learned multimodal reward. Our failed readout and warning audits make this the least attractive immediate route. Base-policy fine-tuning remains a later adaptation baseline; adding it now would change the frozen-policy question and introduce another training budget. These priorities are research judgments, not numerical predictions of success. ([VLA-Pilot](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.14178v2), [local recovery record](docs/research/history/20261019/phase3Recovery.md)).

## Four inexpensive latent extensions

These additions develop the shared-controller direction. Sampling distribution, persistence and response-informed coordinates can be tested within the noise interface; internal activation gating is a distinct intervention with more integration work. All remain hypotheses for ManiGuard. Here latent control uses the frozen policy's representations or sampling inputs, without requiring a new world model.

### Evolve the sampling distribution

Start with a small noise bias and a bounded scale, extending the additive controller to adjust the distribution from which its latent choices are drawn. Restrict changes to the audited active subspace and preserve native sampling elsewhere. Zero bias and unit scale must reproduce the native distribution and RNG path. This adds no policy samples per deployed query. The first question is whether these settings change useful behaviour across development families; lower randomness is not itself safer execution. Noise-distribution steering already has precedent in [DSRL](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.15799v2). Compare native sampling, a small fixed grid and random search before crediting evolution.

### Evolve persistence between policy queries

Test a bounded coefficient controlling how much of a latent choice carries into the next query. Specify whether it retains the compact steering code or correlates the full sampling noise, and align overlapping chunk positions using the actions actually executed. A memoryless setting is the identity reference. Persistence might reduce inconsistent approaches, but can also preserve an ineffective choice or delay a needed change. [EAPN](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.06090v1) studies execution-aligned stochastic structure with joint training and history conditioning; it does not validate our proposed frozen-policy version. Compare fixed persistence values with any conditional rule, and verify that a reset trigger activates before searching it.

### Evolve in coordinates informed by decoder responses

Use bounded forward probes on development observations to find noise directions that substantially change decoded action chunks, then expose a compact response-informed basis to evolution. This reuses the proposed authority audit rather than introducing a dynamics model. [Principal Steering Subspaces](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.33765v1) already constructs such coordinates for RL. Response magnitude measures influence, not useful progress or safety. Compare equally sized random and response-informed bases under matched evolutionary and ordinary-learning budgets. Construct and freeze the basis using development families only; query costs remain charged even when no simulator advances.

### Evolve small internal activation gates

Collect the frozen VLA's internal activations alongside physical safe-completion, violation and unfinished labels. Fit outcome-associated subspaces on development data, then evolve a few bounded gate strengths or combinations while retaining the base weights. These activations are different from our cached DINO features and need their own capture hook. [COAST](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.17144v1) provides an existing activation-gating method and cross-task experiments, making its fixed gate an essential comparator. Outcome association does not establish a causal safety direction. Additional model integration and activation acquisition make this the next distinct avenue, rather than the cheapest first test; transfer between our family-specific checkpoints remains unmeasured.

**Priority:** begin with a small noise-scale and persistence diagnostic, use response-informed coordinates if useful controllability is established, and retain activation gating as the separate follow-up. Two scalar knobs need a grid baseline; their existence alone is not an evolutionary contribution. A shared conditional controller must still outperform fixed settings, random search and ordinary learning on reserved tasks and an adaptation-held-out family.

Declare a finite development allowance and the selected representation before comparative search. These options do not expand the illustrative 64-attempt pilot or consume its protected final audit. Any added fitting, decoder probes, activation capture or physical trials need explicit accounting and a prospective budget within the available allocation. Report safe completion, raw physical violations and unfinished episodes for every physical comparison.

## A compact controller needs an evolutionary explanation

The proposed controller perturbs native Gaussian flow noise using a fixed low-dimensional basis: `z' = epsilon + alpha B tanh(W phi)`. The base VLA, preprocessing, output transforms and native execution cadence remain frozen. **One shared `W` is the generalization target.** Its causal features should combine robot/history information with a common frozen representation of the native images and instruction. This permits task-conditioned responses without Jar-specific predicates or a family one-hot. Reuse of the existing projected visual/text features is a candidate, not a validated semantic interface; feature extraction, normalization, latency and baseline access must be matched. ([Existing shared feature implementation](experiments/helpers/maniguard_phase2_adapter.py)).

A four-feature robot/history prototype with four outputs has 16 weights and is useful for checking integration. It is not the full generalist architecture. Adding shared visual/text conditioning increases its parameter count and preparation cost, which must be frozen before search. Proprioception alone does not identify grasp, task phase or safety. A conditional controller still depends on what the frozen VLA can generate; noise steering does not guarantee safe outputs or a preserved action manifold.

The model's latent width must not be confused with the robot interface. Current pi0.5 family configurations specify a 16-action horizon and 32 model coordinates, while physical output is seven arm joints plus gripper. Audit the loaded shape and the mapping before constructing a basis. Begin with a fixed arm-supported basis, preserve independent native noise on the other coordinates and document whether gripper steering is permitted. Repeating coefficients across a chunk is already used in DSRL and Flow Reversal Steering. Identical tensor shapes do not imply aligned useful latent directions across separately fine-tuned family policies. Check the shared basis on designated development families; family-specific bases fitted from new-family outcomes would weaken the shared-controller claim. At zero weights, preserve the native noise distribution, RNG advancement and numerical inference path; passing a zero noise tensor is a different policy. ([Current configuration](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NU-IDEAS-Lab/ManiGuard/main/maniguard/openpi_sft/train_configs.py), [physical action contract](https://nu-ideas-lab.github.io/ManiGuard/docs/fine_tuning/openpi/), [DSRL](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.15799v2), [Flow Reversal Steering](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.13675v1)).

The hypothesis is that nearby bounded controllers produce useful, partially heritable behavioural differences, allowing retention and mutation to improve adaptation efficiency under noisy episode feedback. Action-space smoothness alone is insufficient evidence: similar decoded chunks can have different contact outcomes. Direct simulator outcomes avoid a learned safety predictor, but remain noisy. A simple established EA should come first, using a frozen transparent objective such as the recovery plan's `int(S_req) - 2 * int(V)`, where `S_req = requested_completion AND NOT V`, with every raw outcome retained. A violating completion therefore scores -2, not -1. This makes no penalty-free claim. A later safety-aware allocation mechanism should track uncertainty, reserve a finite reevaluation budget and compare fixed replication with adaptive racing. Two safe rollouts cannot certify a low violation probability; unresolved candidates must remain unresolved. ([Recovery objective and comparison controls](docs/research/history/20261019/phase3Recovery.md)).

Novelty must be stated narrowly. DSRL already learns state-conditioned noise with a frozen generator; Flow Reversal Steering explicitly studies frozen pi0.5; low-dimensional steering therefore is not the contribution. VLA-Pilot already provides evolutionary frozen-VLA steering at deployment. Online-ES executes perturbed trajectories and updates the action expert through feedback-weighted training, so critic-free evolutionary VLA improvement is also occupied. Our distinguishing experiment would concern a wholly frozen base, a reusable compact controller trained from physical specification outcomes, and an explained safe-completion advantage per total interaction. That combination is a contribution to establish, not proof of priority or novelty merely from combining conventional ingredients. ([DSRL](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v305/wagenmaker25a.html), [Flow Reversal Steering](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.13675v1), [VLA-Pilot](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.14178v2), [Online-ES](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.38855v1)).

## One protected pilot can test authority and actual evolution

**Choose Jar scientifically, then enforce capacity before comparative outcomes.** The published benchmark documents safety as well as completion failures there, and local traces show closure and transport failures. Its 2,000-action horizon and locally observed 2/22 partial safe completions also create a serious floor risk. Low performance does not prove a small controller can repair it. Clutter's 1,100-action horizon and stronger local completion signal make it the declared feasibility fallback. Published Clutter has no measured violations in the main simulation results, so a Clutter gain could primarily concern competence. Published rates and our local rates describe different execution contexts and are not a controlled comparison. ([Benchmark findings](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.17386v1), [local family evidence and horizons](docs/research/history/20261019/phase3.md)).

Before allocation, prepare the explicit-noise hook, accounting and protocol against pinned files. On the host, a bounded decoder audit can use 16 observations from at least four tasks across two designated development families, two native noise draws, and baseline plus positive/negative perturbations along four directions. Reserve at least one whole family before inspecting behavioural responses; source/API shape checks alone need not expose its outcomes. This costs 288 calls; repeating each baseline once gives **320 decoder calls**. Compare same-noise perturbations with native resampling, inspect the complete chunk and actually executed prefix, and log saturation, gripper effects and observation/noise hashes. This tests influence, cross-family interface consistency and reproducibility, not safety or held-out transfer. If each policy requires bespoke steering codes, reconsider native noise as the shared representation before scaling search. Four full development episodes then check identity, native integration and timing. Reserve setup, repair and preservation time, and estimate remaining work using at least `max(180 seconds, 1.25 * slowest development episode)`, increasing it if horizon or initialization timing demands. Choose the family and pilot size before reading comparative search scores. ([Source-backed precursor and costing](docs/research/research_notes/Evolutionary%20improvement%20on%20ManiGuard/benchmark_levers.md), [allocation controls](docs/research/history/20261019/phase3Recovery.md)).

One concrete capacity template is **60 planned valid episodes plus at most four invalid-attempt replacements**. After four development episodes, acquire eight shared initialization episodes: four controllers on two common development cases. Include identity, two declared constant biases and one random conditional controller. Select the fixed reference from the constant biases using only these training observations. Give EA and random search the same initial data, then 16 descendant episodes each: two populations of four controllers on two cases, including charged elite reevaluation. EA retains and mutates; random search samples the same bounded domain. This contains actual evolutionary transitions. Freeze both finalists and the fixed reference before **16 audit episodes**, evaluating native, fixed, EA and random conditions on four common independent checking blocks. Prefer four previously unexposed base tasks, with variants grouped and the original main-study test partition preserved. If exposure history allows only two tasks repeated twice, disclose the narrower scope.

The resulting ceiling is **64 full attempts**, or at most **128,000 Jar policy control actions** before initialization, settling and short engineering work. All partial and failed attempts remain charged. Task timeouts are valid outcomes, never infrastructure replacements. Historical Jar repeats averaged about 270 summed process seconds per episode; 64 times that is 4.8 hours, an illustration that leaves no cold-start guarantee. Setup, decoder calls, audits, idle allocation, failures and preservation all count toward the existing one-GPU eight-hour first-pilot constraint. If conservative timing cannot protect the complete comparison, use the predeclared Clutter feasibility route or report that capacity is insufficient. Do not shorten horizons, change rendering or add rental time to make the arithmetic work. ([Historical Jar timing](docs/research/reports/ManiGuard%20Jar%20guard%20and%20motion%20repeats.md), [resource requirement](docs/research/history/20261019/phase3.md)).

Four audit episodes per condition and one optimizer seed cannot establish superiority. The pilot can reject a broken interface, expose adverse physical effects or provide an effect-size lead. Ordinary learning is explicitly outstanding in this minimum design. Add it only if a complete meaningful arm and the independent audit fit; otherwise the next comparison must supply it. If noise cannot produce relevant bounded alternatives within its engineering allowance, freeze a separate direct-residual study. A negative score alone is not permission to switch routes, discard costs or stop a valid comparison because EA trails random search.

## A paper needs matched learning and reserved evidence

The paper-level baseline set must include contemporaneous native execution, a genuinely tuned constant/fixed correction, matched random search, and ordinary learning with the same observations and output domain. Episodic REINFORCE over the same small parameterization asks whether that particular evolutionary update helps; it does not replace a competent state-conditioned DSRL-style latent SAC baseline. The latter asks whether evolution is practically preferable to established noise-space RL. If claiming that latent steering is a better representation, additionally compare a bounded direct-action residual using appropriate PPO/SAC. Equal care, tuning allowances, shared data, finalist confirmation and final checking matter as much as algorithm names. Dense progress rewards, privileged critic inputs or additional visual encoders change the information endowment and need matched or separately labelled comparisons. ([DSRL](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.15799v2), [ResiP](https://residual-assembly.github.io/), [ResFiT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.19301)).

Separate four conclusions: the interface improves the native checkpoint; conditioning improves a constant bias; evolution improves random and ordinary learning; and the effect generalizes. If fixed bias explains the gain, report that. If adaptation helps but optimizers tie, retain the adaptation result and reject evolutionary superiority. If evaluation allocation is the claimed mechanism, test it against one-shot selection, fixed resampling and matched adaptive racing, with the same total physical allowance and equal finalist protection. A conventional EA can support a strong empirical contribution, but an algorithmic claim needs evidence for its distinctive ingredient. No continuation rule should require an EA win for the experiment to count.

Freeze an independent main-study protocol after the pilot, including a meaningful safe-completion effect, any acceptable violation increase, uncertainty analysis and sample allocation. A superiority claim needs supported positive differences, while safety noninferiority needs its own declared margin and evidence. A nonsignificant violation difference does not establish safety. Replicate optimizer runs and cluster evaluation by base task; repeated frames are not independent trials. With zero violations in `n` independent episodes, the approximate 95% upper bound is `3/n`, not zero. Match interaction ceilings and compare observed cost curves at common control counts, since equal episode counts need not mean equal interactions. Charge discarded candidates, reevaluations, basis-response calls and all training data; report VLA queries and elapsed time separately. ([Repository research controls](AGENTS.md), [recovery evaluation design](docs/research/history/20261019/phase3Recovery.md)).

Generalization needs a separately budgeted bridge after the first-family pilot. One transparent exploratory template is 32 adaptation episodes each for EA, random search and latent learning, balanced over two development families: **96 adaptation episodes**. Freeze one shared controller per method and evaluate those three plus native and fixed references on four new task blocks in each of those families and one adaptation-held-out family: **60 checking episodes, 156 total**, before tuning, confirmation, failures and engineering. At five minutes per episode this is 13 serial rollout hours. One optimizer seed and four task blocks per family remain exploratory, not a power calculation or approved allocation. Use that evidence to budget independent optimizer replication and broader untouched evaluation before a paper claim.

Joint adaptation must balance families and report each family's completion, violations and regressions; an easy-family gain must not conceal Jar deterioration. Distinguish method reuse with per-family refitting, one frozen controller on new tasks from training families, and that same controller on an adaptation-held-out family. Only the latter tests the stronger cross-family target. The held-out family still uses its own pretrained family checkpoint, so this is adapter transfer, not an unseen-family foundation policy. Previously studied families are also not wholly unseen to the research programme. Behavioural-response inspection, basis construction and feature selection count as development exposure. If one shared controller fails but per-family fits work, report the weaker reusable-method result explicitly.

The benchmark contract must remain intact: source revision, assets, native hard horizons, camera mapping, renderer, grasp mode, prompts and checkpoint stay matched. Current documentation defaults to 640-pixel rendering, whereas our earlier execution used a legacy profile; silently mixing them invalidates attribution. Record native success and engagement-conditioned metrics with their denominators, raw physical violations, and the four completion/safety outcomes. Also retain requested-sequence completion, particularly Jar closure, so goal loopholes do not become the contribution. The live monitor supplies training labels by default, not deployed actor inputs. A whole-suite claim eventually needs fixed evaluation and aggregation across the suite, including null families. A reserved Jar improvement is a legitimate narrower achievement; it does not establish domination of every benchmark policy or family. ## Conclusion

The research opportunity is to discover whether the frozen generator contains usable alternative behaviour that a compact evolutionary controller can reliably recover under a real interaction budget. That question is stronger than another optimizer run over mostly inactive execution knobs, and more direct than rebuilding a prediction pipeline before physical adaptation works. The next expenditure should buy evidence about that hypothesis and an independently checked comparison, even if the answer favours a fixed bias, random search or ordinary learning.

The preferred direction is therefore **one shared native-noise controller, tested physically first on Jar, with one audited residual fallback and a capacity-declared Clutter pilot**. The intended contribution requires independent support for its behavioural mechanism, evolutionary advantage and scope, followed by a precise novelty assessment. The result we should seek is an explained improvement whose costs and limitations remain convincing when all unsuccessful candidates and competing methods are visible.
