# Research direction index

**Updated 18 October 2026.** [phase3.md](phase3.md) is the active research plan and
direction. Use [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) for engineering and preservation controls.

The current question is whether evolutionary search can discover a compact execution
controller that improves safe completion around a frozen manipulation policy more
reliably than matched random search and ordinary learning under a fixed interaction
budget. The core study concerns safe execution adaptation. Continuation under a learned
action-changing safety filter requires a separately specified extension.

The phase-three plan records the proposed safety dial, comparisons, experimental gates,
corrected budgets and unresolved decisions. Its main protocol and total run allocation
are not yet frozen. The phase-two pilot remains paused; its historical instructions are
not a request to resume it.
