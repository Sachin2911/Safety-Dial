## What GECCO is

The Genetic and Evolutionary Computation Conference is the main annual conference for
evolutionary computation, run by ACM SIGEVO since 1999. It is ranked CORE A. Accepted full
papers are published in the ACM Digital Library main proceedings.

GECCO 2027 is in **Kraków, Poland, 12 to 16 July 2027**. The official homepage says
"in person only", so an accepted paper means an author travels to present it.

Later routes if the January deadline is missed (2026 dates; expect similar in 2027):

- **Workshop papers and the Student Workshop:** submissions 2 February to 3 April,
  notification 24 April, camera-ready 5 May. The Student Workshop takes 4-page papers with
  a student as main contributor, first author and corresponding author.
- **Late-breaking abstracts:** submissions 2 March to 3 April, notification 28 April,
  camera-ready 5 May.

These, and poster-only papers, appear in the Companion proceedings, not the main
proceedings.

## Submission rules (from the 2026 call)

- **Full paper:** at most 8 pages excluding references, ACM `acmart` `sigconf` format.
  Anything beyond 8 pages goes in supplementary material (at most 10 MB, anonymised).
- **Poster-only paper:** at most 4 pages including references, no abstract step.
- **Review:** double-blind and per track. Papers must be anonymised, including
  self-citations. LaTeX header:
  `\documentclass[dvipsnames,format=sigconf,anonymous=true,review=true]{acmart}`
- **Dual submission:** not allowed. Posting to arXiv is allowed.
- **Generative AI:** any use must be disclosed in the acknowledgements; AI tools cannot be
  authors.
- **Acceptance rate:** 35 to 37% for full papers from 2022 to 2026 (150 of 425 in 2026),
  capped at 40% per track.
- **Tracks (14 in 2026):** BBSR, CS, ECOM, EML, EMO, ENUM, GA, GECH, GP, L4EC, NE, RWA, SI,
  THEORY. The closest to the allocated topic are Neuroevolution (NE), whose scope names
  reinforcement learning, and Evolutionary Machine Learning (EML).

## Costs

- **Publishing fee:** ACM became fully open access in 2026, so authors pay an article
  processing charge unless their institution is in ACM Open. Wits is covered through the
  SANLiC agreement, but the agreement found runs 2024 to 2026. Confirm with the Wits
  library whether it is renewed for 2027.
- **Registration (2026 prices):** student in-person early-bird was USD 475 for members and
  USD 600 for non-members; regular was USD 575 and USD 700.
- **Travel grants (2026):** ACM student grants covered the registration fee only, required
  serving as a student volunteer, gave priority to students with accepted papers, and
  closed on 29 April.
