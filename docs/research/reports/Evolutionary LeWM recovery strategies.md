# Evolve target selection before enlarging residuals

The strongest campaign candidate is a **compact evolved subgoal selector around frozen LeWM**, starting in TwoRoom after an action-contract diagnostic. It addresses a plausible planning bottleneck while exposing only a few persistent parameters to evolution. Keep a structured PushT residual as the parallel alternative. These are experiment recommendations inferred from the literature, not established SafeLeWM results. The failed 510-weight adapters do not show that evolution is unsuitable: their search budget, initialization and training-panel ceiling also matter. A poor arbitrary untrained controller should not veto further learning; require useful controller authority and a panel with room for improvement. The seven-hour campaign can establish an evolved-controller benefit, but an evolutionary optimizer advantage requires the full matched comparison.

## Repair the shared planner before crediting evolution

The closest new evidence is **Aim Short to Reach Far**, a September 2026 preprint studying frozen LeWM on Cube, PushT, Reacher and TwoRoom. It shows that short-horizon scoring against a distant final goal can favor inaction even with exact prediction and globally optimal search. Anchored Planning retrieves a training segment from current/final image encodings and plans toward an early observed successor; physical completion still uses the original final goal. Its improvements are configuration-dependent, and execution timing matters. AP-CEM also clips proposed actions before prediction. ([Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.30036v1))

Our first diagnostic should therefore compare current semantics with a repaired path where prediction, safety checking and execution evaluate the same legal action. Any resulting gain belongs to the **repaired native baseline**, not evolutionary training. Freeze the repair before comparing controllers. Record proposed and executed commands separately and preserve every failed attempt. Fixed timing, correlated sampling and trajectory-memory improvements are meaningful controls: iCEM already demonstrates the value of such search refinements. ([iCEM](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v155/pinneri21a.html))

If distant-goal scoring remains uninformative after the repair, simply evaluating more residual-action candidates attacks the wrong bottleneck. This is a diagnosis to test, rather than a conclusion already established for our new hazard cases.

## Evolve a few persistent choices over observed targets

The proposed selector ranks a **frozen training-only bank of actual observed targets**. A small genotype can control current/source versus final/endpoint similarity weights, successor offset, clearance preference and target-switch persistence. LeWM then searches actions toward the chosen target using its existing frozen representation and safety checker. Reset selector state between episodes and freeze learned weights during evaluation. This produces a reusable evolved policy rather than a newly optimized waypoint sequence for each test episode.

Observed targets avoid inventing unrestricted latent vectors, but they do not prove reachability or safety between observations. Retrieved routes can cross newly introduced hazards; every executed prefix still needs the unchanged learned check and an independent observed-safety monitor. Search on the Replay Buffer supplies a broader precedent for decomposing visual navigation into previously seen subgoals, although it uses a learned goal-conditioned value function rather than frozen LeWM or evolution. ([SoRB](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.05253))

Memory acquisition is real work. AP's official reproduction guide requires external datasets and encoded caches beyond its code release. For this campaign, use a small fixed public training-observation bank if available; otherwise collect a separately frozen common bank on a rented host. Charge simulator preparation and encoder work, exclude confirmation episodes from memory, and give every optimizer the identical cache. Exact final-goal coordinates would be extra policy information unless explicitly declared and shared. ([Official reproduction guide](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/daybraeklaxry/Aim-Short-to-Reach-Far/main/REPRODUCING.md))

If retrieval engineering consumes the useful window, the parallel alternative is an **8-32 coefficient bounded feedback residual** using declared hazard-relative geometry, causal stall feedback and native-plan direction, with a zero/native anchor and a shared temporal action basis. That dimensional range is our engineering choice. Behavior Policy Learning supports restricted high-level targets and small-sigma CMA-ES fine-tuning from a useful imitation initialization; it does not demonstrate discovering the whole controller from scratch. ([BPL](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/robotics-and-ai/articles/10.3389/frobt.2022.974537/full))

## Spend evaluations on learning and confirmation

World Models establishes that persistent controller evolution over learned representations can work. Its 867-parameter CarRacing controller used population 64, averaged candidate fitness over 16 rollout seeds and reached its stated criterion after 1,800 generations. That supports the architecture, not an expectation that 24 genotypes should train our 510-weight adapter. ([World Models](https://worldmodels.github.io/))

Freeze a mixed-success development panel, separate common selection cases and untouched confirmation tasks before evaluating genotypes. Rank observed safety violations and original safe completion before bounded progress or speed tie-breaks. Use common task/seed schedules and several optimizer repeats where feasible. Count all simulator actions, acquisition, discarded candidates, failed starts, model queries and elapsed time. Equal candidate caps alone do not equalize actual work. Black-DROPS motivates checking candidate-rank stability under noisy rollouts, while ARS shows why compact random-search policies and multiple seeds are serious controls. ([Black-DROPS](https://arxiv.org/html/1703.07261v2), [ARS](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2018/hash/7634ea65a4e6d9041cfd3f7de18e334a-Abstract.html))

Compare repaired native planning, a tuned fixed selector, parameter random search, ordinary learning and CMA-ES with the same controller family and available information. Online CEM remains action planning; persistent evolutionary updates must be separately recorded. POPLIN's successful fixed-zero-base planning makes this distinction necessary. ([POPLIN](https://arxiv.org/html/1906.08649))

A confirmed EA-trained policy improvement over native and fixed controls would support an evolved-controller result. If random search or ordinary learning matches it, evolutionary optimizer superiority remains unestablished. Development-only gains remain development signals. The useful paper contribution is the measured behavior of the complete frozen-model controller under fixed safety requirements; neither this focused review nor a seven-hour deadline supplies a novelty or safety guarantee.
