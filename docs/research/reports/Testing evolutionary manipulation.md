# Testing evolutionary manipulation

**I would start with Factory/FORGE peg insertion, establish a repeatable safe-completion result, and then test the same learning procedure on gear meshing without task-specific retuning.** The first result should establish that learning discovers useful contact behaviour. A matched comparison should then identify what evolution contributes, and transfer of the procedure should test the reduced-tuning claim. These are separate scientific achievements. This document is a discussion proposal, not a frozen protocol or execution authorization. It changes neither the active plan nor existing allocations. No experiment, simulator test or rental operation was performed.

**The first benchmark should isolate contact search and recovery.** FORGE studies peg insertion, gear meshing and nut threading with force feedback and variation in contact conditions. Its insertion setup begins with the part already held above the target. Hardware pickup and positioning use a separate controller. This makes it suitable for studying approach, alignment, contact search, retraction and retry, with a narrower scope than complete pick-and-insert manipulation. Gear meshing would provide a second contact geometry while preserving much of the infrastructure. Evidence from both remains evidence within one simulator. [FORGE project](https://noseworm.github.io/forge/); [supplement](https://noseworm.github.io/forge/static/forge_supplementary.pdf).

The initial study should retain the native pose-target interface. The current implementation suppresses roll/pitch action channels, permits other pose adjustments and includes a success-prediction output. Gains are controlled and randomized by the environment. Learning stiffness would therefore be a new interface study. Completion must be checked independently through the benchmark geometry; the policy's success prediction cannot serve as ground truth. [Official FORGE implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/isaac-sim/IsaacLab/main/source/isaaclab_tasks/isaaclab_tasks/contrib/forge/forge_env.py).

This recommendation depends on reproducing a pinned implementation. The official repository contains FORGE despite the project's older code-availability label. An unresolved issue reports a discrepancy between configured hole dimensions and asset geometry; this is a reporter's observation, not a confirmed diagnosis. It makes source and asset inspection important before interpreting failure as a learning problem. [Official registration](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/isaac-sim/IsaacLab/main/source/isaaclab_tasks/isaaclab_tasks/contrib/forge/__init__.py); [geometry issue](https://github.com/isaac-sim/IsaacLab/issues/8850).

ManiSkill's PegInsertionSide is a later independent-simulator candidate or fallback. Its standard task includes grasp acquisition, demonstrations and geometric success evaluation, but would need a declared safety extension. A pregrasp reset would change the benchmark and must be identified as such. Hardware transfer would provide stronger external evidence after simulation results justify the additional work. [ManiSkill task documentation](https://maniskill.readthedocs.io/en/latest/tasks/table_top_gripper/index.html#peginsertionside-v1).

**The safety requirement must be meaningful before any optimizer is compared.** FORGE's native penalty uses a smoothed wrench-derived force signal. That is not automatically a local peg/socket contact force or a raw impact peak. The proposed episode monitor must specify the measured quantity, frame, sampling, filtering, threshold and any sustained-exceedance rule. Qualification should examine reset transients, saturation and sensitivity to the contact solver. These choices must be fixed before comparative outcomes. [FORGE implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/isaac-sim/IsaacLab/main/source/isaaclab_tasks/isaaclab_tasks/contrib/forge/forge_env.py).

The force limit needs a task or part justification. FORGE notes that some assembly tasks require a minimum force and discusses nominal forces from component specifications. A very small threshold can make insertion impossible. If physical damage tolerances are unavailable, the study should explicitly concern compliance with a declared simulated force requirement. Rigid-body benchmark results cannot directly establish prevention of material breakage or human injury. [FORGE paper](https://noseworm.github.io/forge/static/forge_paper.pdf).

**Gate 1 is valid measurement and attainable safe behaviour.** First reproduce the published task competence. Then qualify a competent native controller, a force-aware fixed reference and a useful safe witness using the learner's observations and action interface. A witness with privileged socket pose establishes physical feasibility only. Include a no-motion or stop-only diagnostic to verify that inactivity scores as unfinished. Controlled contact diagnostics should confirm that the monitor detects the intended event. All qualification interactions require their own prospective budget.

Choose the development distribution before observing candidate outcomes. Start with declared pose-estimation errors and initial misalignment; distinguish these from changes to actual geometry. Introduce friction variation as a named challenge. Do not tighten the force boundary until the baseline fails or select cases because they favour evolution. If a competent force-aware reference already meets the safety and utility targets across these conditions without retuning, this benchmark provides little headroom for the proposed reduced-tuning contribution. If no safe witness can act through the interface, stop before optimizer comparison.

**Gate 2 is repeated learned benefit on one task family.** A bounded development screen should compare the untrained or nominal controller, the competent fixed reference, matched parameter random search and evolution. Random search must receive the same candidate representation, observations, action authority and evaluation budget. A shared protective controller should remain identical across methods. Inspect commanded and executed actions and interventions so that improved completion behind a guard is attributed correctly.

As a discussion target, a **10 percentage point absolute gain in safe completion** over the nominal controller would be practically interesting, provided the independently declared risk requirement is met. Five independent development learning runs are a reasonable screening starting point, not a sufficient confirmatory sample size. These numbers are proposed margins requiring justification and freezing before outcomes. A strong reference near the performance ceiling would require a different prospective claim. Consistent learning across repeats is more informative than the best trained policy, and additional evaluation episodes cannot replace independent training runs. [Agarwal et al., 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.13264).

**Gate 3 is independent confirmation against credible alternatives.** Freeze the algorithm settings, selection procedure, monitors, case distributions and analysis before evaluating untouched cases. Include an ordinary learner with explicit constraint handling, such as a compatible adaptive-penalty policy-gradient method whose development baseline has been reproduced. Constrained learning already separates performance from safety requirements, so a poorly chosen fixed penalty would be an inadequate sole comparison. [Constrained Policy Optimization](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/achiam17a.html).

For optimizer attribution, share the policy representation, safety objective and evaluation conditions where possible. If a method receives extra demonstrations, different feedback or another objective, describe the comparison as one between complete methods. Use paired cases and uncertainty estimates reflecting both training runs and task instances. Compare the interval for the difference directly. A supported advantage over nominal establishes learned benefit; a supported advantage over random and the ordinary learner supports an evolutionary comparison. Random search matching evolution still supports a useful searchable controller family, while leaving evolutionary superiority unestablished. [Empirical Design in Reinforcement Learning](https://jmlr.org/papers/v25/23-0183.html).

Report ordinary completion, any-violation frequency, strict safe completion, unfinished episodes and meaningful engagement, all with denominators. Peak force, overload duration and intervention frequency explain outcomes. An early stop or timeout is unfinished unless independent success checks pass; stopping after a violation does not erase it. Record training-time violations separately from final-policy performance. If improvement consists mainly of abandoned tasks, it does not pass the safe-completion gate.

The confirmatory litmus should require a useful safe-completion gain with uncertainty supporting the declared claim, plus a safety upper bound meeting the predeclared risk criterion. An estimate of a ten-point gain with a lower bound above zero supports positive benefit; claiming at least ten points requires the lower bound to exceed ten. Zero observed violations also has uncertainty. For a fixed policy and independent identically distributed episodes, its one-sided 95% bound is approximately 3/n. That calculation does not apply directly to correlated frames or pooled runs from a few selected policies. [NIST exact binomial bounds](https://itl.nist.gov/div898/software/dataplot/refman2/auxillar/exacbino.htm).

**Gate 4 directly tests reduced retuning.** Freeze the controller structure, safety-handling procedure and optimizer settings, then allow fresh learning on a new contact regime or gear meshing under a declared budget. Evaluate on untouched target cases. This tests transfer of the learning recipe. Evaluating the original weights without learning is a separate test of policy robustness; fresh training must not be called zero-shot transfer.

Compare attainment of the same safety and utility targets against a ledger of task-specific settings, reward-weight sweeps, controller modifications and selection evaluations. Record shared development effort too. A proposed five-point safe-completion noninferiority margin against the tuned fixed reference could define acceptable proximity, but it requires independent justification. Its uncertainty bound must remain above the negative margin while the safety criterion also holds. Similar point estimates or an inconclusive difference do not establish noninferiority or reduced tuning.

Count reproduction, witness construction, measurement checks, failed starts, discarded candidates, tuning and selection in the interaction account. Report model calls and elapsed time separately. Choose final sample sizes and budgets from the intended effect, risk criterion and independently estimated variability, then freeze stopping rules. A failed gate should produce a diagnosis and a new prospective proposal, not an automatic expansion of the interface, feedback or allocation.

**The first convincing signal would be repeatable additional safe completion on unseen cases through learned contact behaviour.** The stronger research result would show what evolution adds relative to matched alternatives and that the learning recipe survives a meaningful task change with less condition-specific adjustment. That chain provides a bounded empirical claim about useful safe manipulation. Each subsequent stage should earn its place through the preceding evidence.
