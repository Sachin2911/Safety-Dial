# Preparing a GECCO submission

The project has a credible foundation for a GECCO submission, but its central contribution still needs firmer evidence. My recommendation is a focused empirical paper about **when compact evolutionary adapters recover safe task completion around frozen world-model planners**. TwoRoom can provide controlled diagnosis; Block Plucking can provide manipulation confirmation if a qualified interface produces a learned benefit. The strongest remaining requirement is a consequential result that survives comparison with the frozen planner, untrained adapters and alternative search. Optimizer superiority, reduced manual tuning and calibrated risk are separate claims. Choose one primary claim with Geraud and make every remaining experiment support or challenge it.

**The contribution must go beyond assembling familiar components.** World Models already evolves compact controllers using learned world representations and CMA-ES. Deb's feasibility comparisons already avoid a penalty coefficient, and Safe Neural Evolutionary Strategies already addresses probabilistic safety constraints. These precedents leave room for a specific finding about adapting an existing frozen planner to safety requirements, including acquisition cost and failure conditions. They do not support claiming the general combination as new. The final related-work comparison should identify exactly what your setting, method or finding adds. ([World Models](https://worldmodels.github.io/), [Deb](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0045782599003898), [Safe Neural Evolutionary Strategies](https://arxiv.org/html/2005.03898v2).)

**The decisive experiment must show what learning contributes.** TwoRoom is promising: the matched study records CMA 44/216 safe completions, random search 37/216 and PPO 20/216, but both primary uncertainty intervals include zero. Block Plucking Search 2 records 14/48 sustained safe completions for CMA, 16/48 for an untrained selector and 9/48 for native. The latter comparison makes an untrained structural change a serious alternative explanation. A new final study should compare the selected learned adapter with native, multiple untrained draws, matched random search and a credible ordinary-learning comparator on fresh cases. Report safe completion together with completion, violations, unfinished episodes, engagement and actual interaction cost. ([TwoRoom comparison](../../../SafeLeWM/results/14-matched-ppo.md), [Search 2](../../../SafeBlock/Search2Pooled.md).)

Random search remaining competitive does not invalidate useful adaptation. It limits a CMA-specific superiority claim and makes the explanation of when search helps more important. Likewise, one PPO configuration with sparse terminal reward supports a configuration-specific comparison, not a broad conclusion that reinforcement learning cannot solve the task. Keep information, selection work and simulator-action allowances comparable within each host. Choose task and learner repeats from development variability and a meaningful effect or desired precision, rather than treating a fixed seed count as sufficient. Freeze panels, hypotheses, primary contrasts and stopping rules before confirmation; do not keep extending evaluation until a favorable difference appears.

**One explanatory result would make the paper substantially stronger.** Identify the factor behind the main finding and change it alone. For example, a controlled comparison could test an adapter's authority or its use of a safety signal while preserving the other inputs and budget. The current bundled Block Plucking changes do not establish that representation is the bottleneck. A negative result becomes more valuable when it distinguishes competing explanations or predicts a failure condition. Failed qualification and wide intervals alone do not establish a general limitation of evolution. PushT's failed gate can remain an informative boundary of the tested interface; every planned environment need not succeed. ([PushT qualification](../../../SafeLeWM/results/16-pusht-validated.md).)

**Keep optional claims proportional to their evidence.** Reduced tuning requires a comparable fixed or expert-designed reference, a predeclared acceptable performance margin, and prospective records of human choices and tuning/selection trials. A nonsignificant difference does not establish equivalence, and historical person-hours cannot be reconstructed reliably. A calibrated risk dial requires a method designed for the requested limits, independent risk assessment and suitable uncertainty treatment. The completed fixed-utility library does not supply that result. I would keep the dial outside the main contribution unless you deliberately choose the larger constraint-handling study instead. All conclusions should remain scoped to the tested simulator tasks. ([Dial record](../../../SafeLeWM/results/17-safety-dial-library.md).)

**A workable schedule starts with a December decision point.** During November, agree a one-page claim-and-evidence outline with Geraud, identify the closest competing work, and resolve one bounded development question. By early to mid-December, decide whether the evidence supports a useful-recovery paper or a controlled empirical-limits paper. If neither has a convincing central result, consider the poster route rather than expanding the matrix indefinitely. Use the rest of December for prospectively frozen confirmation and the essential explanatory comparison. Aim for a complete draft and reproducibility package in early January, leaving revision time. This is a proposed sequence; new experiments still require their own protocol and authorization.

**The official submission dates are now confirmed:** the mandatory abstract is due **18 January 2027**, and the full paper **25 January 2027**. Full papers allow eight pages excluding references and a maximum 200-word abstract; anonymous ACM sigconf formatting applies. A four-page poster-only paper, including references, is also due 25 January. No deadline timezone was stated on the retrieved dates page. ([Official dates](https://gecco-2027.sigevo.org/Important%2BDates), [call](https://gecco-2027.sigevo.org/Call%2Bfor%2BPapers), [submission instructions](https://gecco-2027.sigevo.org/Paper%2BSubmission%2BInstructions).)

The 2027 call expects runnable code for code-centric results through a new reproducibility pilot. Assemble anonymous code, pinned dependencies and model provenance, frozen configurations, scoring and analysis commands, and an actual recovery route for the central evidence. The local deletion record does not establish that every deleted artifact has a backup. Reconcile the manuscript's outdated Search 2 status before final reporting. The call also requires detailed Methods disclosure of AI involvement in research execution and acknowledgment of writing assistance, and prohibits wholesale AI-generated submission text. Sachin should personally develop and verify the manuscript while accurately documenting assistance. ([Official call](https://gecco-2027.sigevo.org/Call%2Bfor%2BPapers), [repository authority](../../../firstPrinciples.md).)

Neuroevolution is a natural track if evolving neural adapters remains central; Evolutionary Machine Learning is another plausible fit. The immediate milestone is a supervisor-agreed contribution sentence, the result needed to substantiate it, and the result that would falsify it. That makes the remaining work finite and the eventual paper easier to assess. ([NE scope](https://gecco-2027.sigevo.org/Track?itemId=59), [EML scope](https://gecco-2027.sigevo.org/Track?itemId=53).)
