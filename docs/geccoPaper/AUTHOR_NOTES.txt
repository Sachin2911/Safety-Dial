Scientific interpretation
The primary result is a real-minus-imagined failure-gap contrast, not false-safe
acceptance conditional on a safety filter. The noise scale is fixed per latent
coordinate from development residuals, not state-dependent uncertainty. The
penalty adapts observed branch-return extrema, not exact state-value extrema or
a proven minmax bound. The combined method mitigates search-induced harm but
remains worse than the starting controller. The paper preserves failed historical
gates, source-distribution conditioning, unequal model sampling, post-failure
progress, and incomplete upstream cost accounting. No new scientific queries
were performed to write the paper. Source analysis and locked experiments remain
unchanged.

The new evidence replaces the earlier blanket claim that the readout works and
only dynamics are at fault. Eventual segment detection differs from detection
at the first failure-containing endpoint; the real-image probe is imperfect at
onset. Replaying exact real actions misses nearly every first unsafe endpoint,
while frequent real-history refresh partly restores detection. The separate
predictor repair improves one-step audit detection from 11.3% to 67.7%, with
false alarms rising from 0.39% to 0.85%. All three fitting seeds are retained.
The repaired-model nominee fails 3/24 real audit branches versus baseline 2/24,
so the paper claims no controller improvement or promotion. The repaired model
was not used in a new evolutionary search. Its audit is held out within the
repair procedure but was exposed by earlier diagnostics, not fresh confirmation.

The main study remains the prospectively locked result. Follow-up engineering
gates and source-episode bootstrap intervals are separate and exploratory.
The next proposed experiment is a controller-risk ranking audit, not a result
already obtained. Fitting seeds share the same pretrained model and do not
establish generalization to independently trained world models.
