# Recent work closes pilot, redirects research

The 24 commits from `1432288` through `07a7e8c` turned a partially reported ManiGuard native-noise study into a completed, independently checked and portable negative pilot. **The main physical result is clear: the native-noise mechanism executed as designed, but no protected-check condition achieved safe requested completion, evolution did not outperform native execution, and a separate four-start continuation screen failed its advancement gate.** The engineering result is considerably stronger: the repository now has fail-closed execution, complete interaction accounting, exact evidence replay, restoration tooling, bound paper assets and a portable 90-receipt preservation catalog. Decoder and saved-trace analyses identify plausible missing authority around gripper control, persistence and closure-to-holding transitions, but these are diagnostic or model-only findings, not demonstrated physical fixes. The completed studies should not be resumed because their budgets are exhausted and the continuation gate failed. The next defensible move is to freeze a new, small development protocol that tests useful control authority and stability before another optimizer comparison, with matched fixed, random and ordinary-learning controls reserved for any subsequent physical study.

## Executive summary: execution is complete, efficacy is not

The recent range contains **177 changed files, 32,726 insertions and 312 deletions**, including 17 new helper modules, seven new scripts, 14 new test modules, a hardened checkpoint entry point, 25 continuation records and 94 latent-evolution records. Most of that volume is not new controller logic. It is the evidence and control plane needed to freeze, execute, recover, audit and publish the original pilot and the separate continuation screen ([range comparison](https://github.com/Sachin2911/Safety-Dial/compare/1432288...07a7e8c)). The scientific work now has a definite endpoint: **60 valid original episodes, 16 protected checking episodes, four valid continuation-screen episodes and six earlier engineering starts**, totaling 120,131 controls and 15,019 physical policy requests, with zero invalid original or screen full starts ([pilot closeout](../configs/maniguard/latent-evolution/pilot-closeout-20261020.json)).

| Evidence layer | Current status | Defensible interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Physical execution | Complete for the frozen Jar pilot and four-start screen | The tested controller, objective and budget did not produce safe requested completion or an EA advantage |
| Decoder and model-only evidence | Complete for the bounded authority and world-model audits | Native noise affects decoded actions, stronger gripper coordinates can affect commands, and the tested world model failed qualification; none establishes physical benefit |
| Engineering and preservation | Strongly complete for the recorded studies | Execution, accounting and saved evidence are unusually auditable and restorable |
| Current launch readiness | Not established on this checkout | Two local tests lack the licensed Jar scene and ManiGuard checkout; no new run is authorized |
| Future method work | Proposed and partly implemented at CPU level | Progress fitness, persistence, gripper-capable steering and response-informed coordinates still need a new frozen protocol and physical evaluation |

## Physical execution rejects this configuration, not the research avenue

The native-noise pilot did achieve its most basic implementation goal. The shared 176-parameter controller conditioned a bounded four-dimensional noise offset on robot state, executed-action history and frozen image and text features while keeping the VLA, transforms, horizons and monitor behavior fixed. A 320-call decoder audit passed all 32 same-noise identity checks and showed measurable arm-target changes in both Jar and Clutter. Those measurements establish that the interface has influence over decoded behavior. They do not show that the influence improves safety or completion ([pilot report](../docs/research/reports/ManiGuard%20latent%20evolution%20pilot.md)).

The frozen Jar comparison then ran to completion. Across all **40 comparison-training episodes, every episode was requested-unfinished, 16 violated, and four safely achieved supported lid closure without progressing to holding**. Generation one gave both EA and random search population utility `-8`; generation two gave both `-4` on a common panel. Random search produced one raw completion and holding event in generation two, but only after loss of uprightness and without closure, so it remained unsafe and outside the requested sequence ([fitness information audit](../configs/maniguard/latent-evolution/fitness-information-audit-20261020.json); [generation-two summary](../configs/maniguard/latent-evolution/generation-2-summary-20261020.json)).

Protected checking was equally decisive for the tested configuration. Each of native, fixed, EA and random received four reserved episodes, and **all four conditions scored 0/4 safe requested completions**. Native and EA each had one safe unfinished and three unsafe unfinished episodes, three violations and one unsafe raw goal. Fixed and random each had two safe unfinished and two unsafe unfinished episodes, two violations and no raw goal. Fixed and random used exactly the same retained genotype, so their equality is not evidence that two distinct optimizers generalized equally. EA and native were equal in every paired outcome contrast ([independent original60 assessment](../configs/maniguard/latent-evolution/independent-original60-assessment-20261020.json); [paired summary](../configs/maniguard/latent-evolution/original-60-paired-summary-20261020.json)).

The separate continuation screen tested whether a successful closure prefix could be composed with native continuation on one exposed Jar case. It froze four modes before execution: native-only, closure-only, a clock handoff at control 488 and a privileged supported-closure handoff with a three-observation debounce. All four valid starts reached 2,000 controls, for 8,000 controls and 1,000 physical VLA requests. Native-only and closure-only were safe unfinished; both handoff modes were unsafe unfinished. There were no requested completions, raw goals, native holding events or qualifying post-switch milestones, so the larger stage was not admitted ([frozen screen](../configs/maniguard/continuation/handoff-screen-frozen-v1.json); [screen summary](../configs/maniguard/continuation/screen-summary-20261020.json)).

The event timing is diagnostic but not causal. Closure-only remained nonviolating through 1,405 closed observations without holding. The clock trial first violated at control 475, before its switch at 488, so the later handoff cannot explain that first violation. The privileged trial switched at 448 and first violated at 471, while the jar was still closed and supported, but temporal order does not identify a unique action-specific cause. The trace-bound gripper audit further found that native and closure-only always commanded opening, while the two handoff modes issued closing inputs only after their first violations. Finger-joint coordinates later approached zero, but they are not a fingertip-aperture or body-link grasp witness ([independent screen assessment](../configs/maniguard/continuation/independent-screen-assessment-20261020.json); [gripper audit](../configs/maniguard/continuation/screen-gripper-audit-20261020.json)).

The strongest conclusion is consequently narrow but useful: **the tested random-basis, memoryless controller, sparse objective, two-case feedback panels and one optimizer run did not recover safe Jar completion under the frozen budget.** This falsifies the useful form of that configuration. It does not establish that native-noise steering is generally ineffective, estimate a safety rate, demonstrate irreversibility, or distinguish among missing base-policy competence, insufficient controller authority, unstable transitions and weak fitness information. Four checking blocks per condition, one optimizer seed, no competent ordinary-learning arm and no cross-family transfer test are not enough for optimizer-superiority or generalization claims ([paper limitations](../docs/research/reports/maniguard-phase3-part1-gecco/native-noise-paper.tex); [pilot closeout](../configs/maniguard/latent-evolution/pilot-closeout-20261020.json)).

## Model-only evidence diagnoses gaps but validates no remedy

Two saved-input diagnostics sharpen the next question. The original bounded authority audit demonstrated that native-noise perturbations change decoded arm targets at modest magnitudes. A later gripper-coordinate audit found no gripper-command changes at magnitude 0.5, but changes in 3/4 Jar frames and 4/4 Clutter frames at magnitude 2.0, accompanied by arm-target differences. Three inputs were already drawn from violating factual trajectories, and no Jar input demonstrated a successful closure transition. This supports a candidate representation with direct gripper authority, but **it remains decoder-only evidence** and cannot be treated as safe physical repair ([paper diagnostic](../docs/research/reports/maniguard-phase3-part1-gecco/native-noise-paper.tex)).

The CPU world-model audit was correctly rejected for deployment. Model-to-persistence RMSE ratios were 1.973 for robot and history, 1.538 for overview and 1.164 for wrist against a required maximum of 0.90. Actual-feature readout MAE ranged from 0.191 to 0.662 against a 0.10 gate, calibration had one episode against a minimum of 20, and conservative abstention rejected every prefix. The model reused existing data, caused no new physical interaction and never entered physical action selection ([model follow-up](../docs/research/reports/maniguard-phase3-part1-gecco/model-followup.tex)). These failures separately identify weak imagined dynamics and weak readouts. They do not establish that all model-assisted control will fail.

The new progress objective is similarly prospective. It scores complete fixed panels with `F=(4N+1)sum(U)+sum(S)`, preserves the primary integer utility ordering and zeroes secondary progress after any whole-episode violation. Applied retrospectively, it would have changed the factual preferred panel in two of five observed cohorts. That is evidence that the old sparse fitness discarded useful ordering information, not evidence that a different search would have succeeded. The helper passed software checks, but it was not connected to the completed optimizer, actor or physical screen, and dispatch remains unauthorized ([progress-fitness helper](../experiments/helpers/maniguard_progress_fitness.py); [prototype receipt](../configs/maniguard/continuation/progress-fitness-prototype-20261020.json)).

## Validation is strong for history, incomplete for a new launch

The saved-study validation is unusually mature. Independent recovery covers all 60 original episodes and all four screen episodes, including **112,095 and 8,004 exact native monitor entries**, respectively. Recovery started from redownloaded SHA-256-checked archives and performed no new physics or neural work. The final portable catalog embeds 90 byte-verified receipts, and a private round trip restored 639 indexed paper and analysis artifacts ([original60 proof](../configs/maniguard/latent-evolution/original-60-physical-recovery-proof.json); [screen-four proof](../configs/maniguard/continuation/screen-four-physical-recovery-20261020.json); [paper recovery](../configs/maniguard/continuation/final-paper-analysis-recovery-20261020.json)). This materially lowers the risk that the negative result came from a report export, dropped policy response or accounting mismatch. It does not add experimental samples or reduce sampling uncertainty.

Recorded software validation includes a 137-pass continuation suite with Ruff clean, a 21-case independent integration review plus 15 copied-source probes over all 34 frozen files, and a 24-case combined restoration-source recheck ([software receipt](../configs/maniguard/continuation/software-preparation-v2-20261020.json); [integration review](../configs/maniguard/continuation/independent-integration-review-20261020.json)). The fresh repository audit reports **163 passed, two failed and one skipped** across the 14 new test modules, with Ruff clean over all changed Python files. Both failures occurred before their tested logic because the local checkout lacks the licensed Jar scene and `third_party/ManiGuard`; the skipped policy test had previously passed seven cases in the intended policy interpreter. The rerun also used Python 3.11 rather than the repository-requested 3.12 tools environment. These results are consistent with missing documented prerequisites, but they do not establish live simulator or policy readiness on this machine ([admission test](../experiments/tests/test_maniguard_continuation_admission.py); [results test](../experiments/tests/test_maniguard_continuation_results.py); [environment contract](../configs/maniguard/latent-evolution/README.md)).

Two limitations remain operationally important. First, a fresh host still needs pinned private model and dataset archives, exact ManiGuard and OpenPI revisions, BEHAVIOR and robot assets, the CLIP asset, licensed bundles and host-specific NVIDIA and EGL validation. Second, the final allocation snapshot recorded 45.4608 allocated GPU-hours and a USD 13.9245 rate-based upper estimate while the historical instance was still marked running. It was not a provider shutdown or final billing confirmation, so eventual stop state, storage charges and final invoice remain outside the scientific record ([final allocation handoff](../configs/maniguard/continuation/final-allocation-handoff-20261020.json)). Neither issue justifies resuming a completed queue.

## The next protocol should test authority before optimization

The work remains aligned with the current direction because it completed the native-noise pilot honestly, kept the monitor passive, excluded the failed world model, preserved proposed and executed actions, charged all interactions and stopped the continuation study when its prospective gate failed. The completed actor also matches much of the intended architecture: a shared observation- and instruction-conditioned controller over frozen family policies. It does not yet meet the contribution standard because it lacks safe-completion gain, EA advantage, competent ordinary learning and transfer evidence. Treating the privileged screen as the new main method, calling the monitor a learned filter, or launching more trials under an expired protocol would break that alignment ([current direction](../Evolutionary%20improvement%20on%20ManiGuard.md); [continuation README](../configs/maniguard/continuation/README.md)).

| Priority | Concrete action | Evidence gate before proceeding |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Freeze a development-only authority protocol covering gripper-capable noise coordinates, bounded persistence, exact features, resets, cases, seeds, saved-input decoder calls, physical starts and stop rules | Same-noise identity remains exact, perturbations reliably affect the intended decoded controls, and the mechanism activates within the declared finite allowance |
| 2 | Test transition competence on development evidence using the progress-sensitive objective without changing the monitor into a veto | Demonstrate a stable approach-to-grasp or closure-to-holding transition with completion, violations and unfinished episodes reported together |
| 3 | Compare simple explanations before claiming evolutionary value | Include native, a tuned fixed or grid baseline and matched random or local search because noise scale and persistence are simple knobs |
| 4 | If authority passes, freeze a new physical comparison with an actually implemented ordinary-learning arm | Match observations, output domain, information access and total interaction accounting across EA, random search and ordinary learning |
| 5 | Reserve independent checking and transfer before inspecting development outcomes | Keep fresh task blocks and an adaptation-held-out family untouched; replicate optimizer seeds before any superiority claim |
| 6 | If authority fails within its cap, activate a predeclared bounded direct-action residual fallback | Do not enlarge the failed hard-handoff cohort or spend more search budget on an inactive representation |

The immediate blocker is therefore **scientific authorization, not unfinished engineering**. A new protocol must still fix the coordinate basis, gripper authority, persistence semantics, objective, ordinary-learning comparator, task and family partitions, episode and query ceilings, resource window and advancement criteria. Only after that freeze should the environment be reconstructed on a suitable host. Response-informed coordinates should enter only after useful controllability is established, with every decoder probe charged. Internal activation gates should remain a later, separately budgeted avenue because they require new model hooks and have not been tested in ManiGuard ([current direction](../Evolutionary%20improvement%20on%20ManiGuard.md)).

## Conclusion

The recent work changes the project from an incomplete promising experiment into a credible negative result with unusually strong provenance. That is genuine progress: it rules out the tested controller, objective and budget as a path to Jar safe completion, while preserving enough causal structure to identify the next uncertainty. The bottleneck is no longer whether the experiment ran correctly. It is whether a compact shared intervention can produce stable, task-relevant authority before optimization tries to exploit it.

The best next expenditure should therefore buy discrimination among failure modes. A bounded authority-and-persistence study can determine whether gripper control and temporal consistency unlock the closure-to-holding transition. If it does, a matched physical comparison can ask the evolutionary question. If it does not, the project should switch through a predeclared fallback instead of mistaking more search for a diagnosis.
