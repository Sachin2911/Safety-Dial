# Evolutionary opportunities for safe robotics

**Research screening, 16 October 2026. No new direction has been selected.**

**The strongest next bet is to find a consequential missing capability before choosing an evolutionary method.** Our existing UNISafe stack makes confidently missed physical failures and safety-data coverage the most accessible substantive hypothesis. Safe composition of competent manipulation skills offers broader published headroom, with substantially higher setup costs. An offline temporal-warning audit is the cheapest diagnostic, but already faces strong genetic-programming and sequence-learning precedents. These are different rankings: cheap diagnosis does not imply the strongest eventual contribution. The useful lesson from the grasp study is that a large improvement can come from one simple correction, while leaving the proposed safety-filter mechanism and evolutionary advantage unsupported. We should preserve that result and use it to choose problems more carefully.

The local evidence is unusually helpful for this decision. Evolution improved completion from 2/64 to 54/64, but a fixed rule, random search and categorical policy gradient matched or exceeded its completion count. Most improvement persisted without filtering. Evolution had seven physical failures, one boundary exit and two timeouts, compared with six physical failures and 56 timeouts for baseline; unchanged safety risk was not established. The policy-gradient comparison learned only within the same restricted adapter family, so it does not settle what ordinary task-policy learning could achieve. The study establishes useful grasp maintenance on the migrated stack, rather than filter-induced continuation failure or an evolutionary advantage. [Completed local study](UNISafe%20grasp%20maintenance%20and%20evolutionary%20adaptation.md).

Your low-hanging-fruit principle translates into **large, repairable headroom with an identifiable cause**. A weak baseline alone is insufficient: completion can be low because the task policy is incompetent, information is missing, predictions are wrong, safety obligations are ignored or the task is infeasible. Evolution has different relevance to each. Discrete controller structure, complementary behavior collections and diverse counterexamples give it a defensible role. Nondifferentiability alone does not: ordinary RL can learn through a filter, while random search, Bayesian optimization and simple rules also handle black-box objectives.

UNISafe provides a useful example of problem selection, with a qualification. It addressed optimistic latent predictions outside limited training coverage by combining uncertainty estimation, calibration and reachability-based intervention. OOD detection was already an occupied field; the contribution concerned a concrete integration weakness. Its paper also acknowledges imperfect dynamics, unreliable filtering for entirely different tower configurations and the absence of universal safety guarantees. Our nominal experiment neither refutes that contribution nor establishes that learned filtering is generally solved. [UNISafe](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.00779).

Newer work identifies distinct remaining holes. *When World Models Lie* studies low ensemble disagreement despite observed model error. *How Well Do Latent World Models Understand Partially Observable Safety Constraints?* separates recognizing unsafe states from predicting action consequences: its wax experiment reports safe lifts in 3/20 RGB trials versus 20/20 with multimodal inputs or privileged multimodal supervision. These are results in different systems, not explanations of our tower failures. They motivate distinguishing coverage, observability and deployment mismatch before developing a repair. [Model mismatch](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.34300), [partial observability](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.06492).

**1. Diverse confidently missed failures, followed by budget-matched safety-model repair.** This is the strongest local evolutionary hypothesis. The missing capability would be coverage of several physically distinct hazards that the current predictor confidently misses. The question becomes: can acquiring diverse predictive counterexamples repair safety assessment more efficiently than collecting redundant failures or selecting only uncertain trajectories? This directly connects world models, manipulation and safety, without depending on filtering having caused poor completion.

The inexpensive premise check starts with existing physical failures and matched non-failures. Measure the predictor's missed-failure rate at a common false-alarm rate and useful warning horizon. Then use a modest stratified or space-filling sweep over valid scene and contact variations to determine whether errors recur across multiple solvable mechanisms. Separate confidently wrong prediction, justified uncertainty, missing visual information and ineffective backup control. A scripted successful witness on selected scenes helps establish feasibility; unsuccessful finite search does not prove impossibility. The original Hard configuration changes several variables together and has commented-out termination callbacks, so it cannot serve as an isolated robustness comparison without a validated outcome contract. [Pinned Hard configuration](https://github.com/CMU-IntentLab/UNISafe/blob/ab0fdf87f38edefa48b54a291ec5b7156c17b6e0/takeoff/hard_takeoff_env_cfg.py).

An evolutionary object could be a quality-diversity archive of bounded scenario or short-probe parameters, indexed by audited contact geometry, support conditions and failure mechanism. Its quality would reflect verified predictive blind spots, while diversity discourages repeated discovery of one failure. A second, narrower object is sampling proportions over fixed trajectory strata for a frozen-encoder safety head. These are proposed representations, not validated methods. Generic MAP-Elites manipulation scenario generation already exists; DataMIL already studies robotics trajectory selection, and RENEW studies dynamics repair with directed queries in non-manipulation domains. [QD scenario generation](https://roboticsproceedings.org/rss17/p036.pdf), [DataMIL](https://robin-lab.cs.utexas.edu/datamodels4imitation/), [RENEW](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.14180).

The strongest comparisons include random and stratified collection, uncertainty acquisition, concentrated CEM or Bayesian optimization, and greedy feature-diverse selection from the same pool. For repair, include class-balanced data and ordinary supervised updates at equal training cost. **The contribution would be explaining why confidently wrong, physically diverse examples repair held-out safety behavior better per total cost.** Archive coverage alone is insufficient: Q-DIG's diverse instruction attacks already illustrate that greater coverage need not yield the best downstream performance. Reject this bet if simple calibration or a few random examples close the gap, if search finds invalid scenes or rendering artifacts, or if benefits disappear on unseen parameter combinations. A repaired classifier is not automatically a repaired world model or reachability filter; closed-loop physical outcomes must establish the practical gain. [Q-DIG](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.12510).

**2. Temporal safety and composition across competent primitives.** This offers the strongest broader problem headroom, provided component competence can be established. A controller can execute each skill successfully yet fail to preserve an obligation across their composition: maintain support while releasing, clear an obstruction before insertion, or restore a fixture before closing. A next-action filter can already use recurrent latent history, as UNISafe does, without necessarily capturing these task-level obligations. The missing capability would be managing safety-relevant prerequisites and off-nominal transitions across stages.

ManiGuard reports overall safe completion of 7.5-29.8% across its evaluated fine-tuned policies, with Cabinet and Dusty below 2%. It provides physics-grounded temporal specifications independent of completion. However, Clutter has no observed violations despite variable completion, so low success cannot uniformly be called a safety deficit. SafeManip similarly reports greater per-trigger risk in long than atomic task groups, while warning that privileged simulator predicates are not sensor-grounded hardware monitors. These findings justify examining temporal safety, rather than assuming every low-performing task has an evolutionary solution. [ManiGuard](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.17386v1), [SafeManip](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.12386).

The decisive first comparison is between individual skills started from valid states, their full sequence, and one hand-authored safe controller. If the parts work but composition fails, an evolved finite-state controller or behavior tree could search primitive choice, guarded transitions and bounded retries. Discrete structure and delayed consequences provide a plausible evolutionary rationale. Deterministic prerequisite logic, automaton-based shielding, task-and-motion planning and ordinary policy learning with the same monitor are serious alternatives. The nearest evolutionary precedents already include compact multi-stage manipulation policies and model-based skill repertoires. **Reject an evolutionary composition story if a short scripted controller generalizes broadly or nearly all errors occur within one primitive.** [Behavior Policy Learning](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/robotics-and-ai/articles/10.3389/frobt.2022.974537/full), [QDAC](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/grillotti24a.html).

ManiGuard releases monitor/evaluation code and task-family checkpoints, including SmolVLA models. This makes it a stronger candidate foundation than an unimplemented benchmark proposal. It also pins OmniGibson and Isaac Sim 4.5, whereas our accepted runtime uses Isaac Sim 6.2. Checkpoint availability does not establish inference fit or throughput on our 3090. SafeManip's README warns about required gitignored forks and stale installation instructions. This route therefore carries environment, policy, monitor and sensor-interface integration costs before its apparent headroom becomes locally usable evidence. [ManiGuard code](https://github.com/NU-IDEAS-Lab/ManiGuard), [evaluated checkpoints](https://huggingface.co/collections/IDEAS-Lab-Northwestern/maniguard-evaluated-vla-checkpoints), [SafeManip release](https://github.com/chengyuehuang511/SafeManip).

**3. Actionable temporal warnings from deployed histories.** This is the cheapest conditional diagnostic, rather than the strongest novelty claim. Persistent slipping or instability might be visible across time before any instantaneous score becomes alarming. The question is whether available histories contain advance information that a simple current-score threshold misses, and whether intervention remains effective when that warning appears.

Compare a threshold, hysteresis or moving average, logistic regression over history and a small recurrent predictor using identical signals. Measure event recall against per-episode false alarms and required warning lead time. Genetic programming becomes relevant only if meaningful headroom remains: it can search compact past-time formulas, operators and windows. But Brunello and colleagues already evolve temporal failure-detection rules, while Foresight already trains action-conditioned temporal predictors from world-model features. A contribution would need useful physical warning, compactness or transfer beyond these alternatives, not merely GP monitoring. Reject it if a persistence rule matches performance, useful signals appear only after violation, or all predictive information comes from privileged state. A scripted abort or regrasp must subsequently improve physical outcomes before an offline predictor is called a safety improvement. [GP monitoring](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.17786), [Foresight](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.23085).

This audit can begin without new simulation only if sufficient recordings exist. Our controller logs safety outputs and distinguishable actions, but full cameras are conditional and latent histories are not universally saved. Recorded residuals after execution cannot retrospectively warn about the same action's consequence. Upstream pre-failure relabeling and terminal-frame substitution also require independent onset labels. Existing files provide a starting point, not a verified complete monitoring dataset. [Controller](../../../experiments/helpers/unisafe_controller.py), [worker](../../../experiments/scripts/unisafe_adaptation_worker.py), [inference implementation](../../../experiments/scripts/unisafe_inference.py).

Active perception and recovery portfolios remain worthwhile conditional branches. For perception, compare ordinary views, one extra fixed view, scripted inspection and a privileged-information reference. If extra information does not improve control, perception lacks support as the bottleneck; if a fixed view suffices, evolved sensing adds little. ActiveArena demonstrates substantial task headroom and releases models, but supplies no independent physical-safety objective, and moved views can invalidate the current encoder. For recovery, first compare a small scripted bank with its best individual and an oracle selector. Little oracle advantage rules out a useful portfolio-diversity premise. Recova already demonstrates strong ordinary recovery learning, so learning recoveries is an occupied area. [ActiveArena](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.24124), [Recova](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01178).

ROSARL remains optional. Its original result concerns optimal policies in an undiscounted stochastic-shortest-path setting with bounded rewards, absorbing outcomes and proper-policy assumptions. Unsafe terminal labels are supplied externally; minimizing their probability need not make it zero. The practical implementation tracks supplied reward/value extrema. Neither component predicts hidden physical hazards or guarantees safe intermediate exploration. A focused finite-budget study could ask whether rare failures or critic error undermine that estimator, beginning in an inexpensive environment with a swept/exact reference and simple replay. Evolutionary penalty tuning is a weaker bet: confidence-aware safe neuroevolution, PID multiplier adaptation and safety-first curricula already provide close alternatives. No measured ROSARL-specific manipulation deficit currently justifies integration. [ROSARL](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.00035v1), [implementation](https://github.com/geraudnt/rosarl/blob/main/learning_minmax_penalty.py), [safe neuroevolution](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scico.2021.102620), [PID Lagrangian](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/stooke20a/stooke20a.pdf).

The practical recommendation is **a low-cost mechanism audit on current evidence, alongside a narrow feasibility inspection of one temporal-composition task**. Those investigations answer different questions: whether our present assets contain repairable predictive blind spots, and whether a new benchmark offers a stronger missing capability. Neither commits us to UNISafe, a particular optimizer or a fixed experiment budget. If local errors are mostly basic policy weakness or inaccessible information, moving systems is more defensible than manufacturing extreme perturbations to obtain an EA win.

For any chosen study, charge acquisition of the entire trajectory pool, validity checks, discarded candidates, witness episodes and all model training to the budget. Report simulator interactions, model queries and elapsed time separately. Group trajectories sharing starts across adapters before splitting; inspected reserved traces are now development evidence for new hypotheses. Keep runtime sensors matched, physical safety definitions fixed, and proposed versus executed actions distinct. Search yield is not deployment risk: final rates require untouched draws with counts, denominators and uncertainty. Report safe completion, physical violations and unfinished episodes together; zero violations in independent episodes supports an approximate 95% upper bound of 3/n, rather than zero risk. The desired contribution is a demonstrated missing capability and an economical repair that survives strong alternatives.
