# Recent ManiGuard progress audit

Repository-only audit of `1432288..07a7e8c`, reconciled against the current
ManiGuard direction. "Completed" below means completed in the preserved study record.
It does not imply that a new run is currently authorized or that the historical
remote instance is still available.

## Protocol, comparison and budget decisions

### Takeaway

The recent work completed the previously frozen Jar native-noise pilot without
changing its actor, objective, cases or protected checking set. It then froze and
executed a separate four-start continuation capability screen. That screen was a
bounded engineering follow-up, not a revision of the original comparison and not a
new learned safety-filter study.

### Cited Findings

- The primary research direction remains a shared observation- and
  instruction-conditioned controller over family-specific frozen policies, with
  native flow-noise steering as the first bet. Jar is the first physical target and
  Clutter is the capacity fallback. The required paper-level controls are native,
  a strong fixed correction, matched random search and ordinary learning, followed
  by independent evaluation and full interaction accounting. The direction document
  explicitly says that this recommendation is prospective and does not itself launch
  an experiment. [Source](../../Evolutionary%20improvement%20on%20ManiGuard.md)

- The original pilot retained a 60-valid-episode design under a 64-full-attempt cap.
  Its completed structure was four development episodes, eight shared initialization
  episodes, 32 descendant-training episodes and 16 protected checking episodes.
  Independent validation reports 60 expected jobs, 60 verified jobs, 60 full
  attempts, no errors, no pending jobs and the frozen wave order intact.
  [Source](../../configs/maniguard/latent-evolution/design-verification-original60-20261020.json)

- The four checking conditions were native, fixed, EA and random. Each received four
  reserved cases. Fixed and random ended with the identical retained genotype, which
  makes their equality a factual identity rather than evidence that two distinct
  optimizers generalized equally. The six paired contrasts were retained
  descriptively and no winner was selected after seeing checking outcomes.
  [Source](../../configs/maniguard/latent-evolution/original-60-paired-summary-20261020.json)

- The continuation follow-up was prospectively frozen at four total charged starts,
  with partial failures charged, no automatic replacements and no extra decoder
  probes. It fixed `random-g1-2`, task `task_0019/base`, seed `6101920`, clock 488 and
  debounce 3 before dispatch. Its four modes were native-only, closure-only,
  clock-handoff and privileged supported-closure handoff. The monitor remained a
  logger, not an action veto, and the failed world model was not deployed.
  [Source](../../configs/maniguard/continuation/handoff-screen-frozen-v1.json)

- The screen's advancement rule required new ordered target holding, lift or
  transport strictly after a real handoff, or requested safe completion first
  achieved after switching, with no whole-episode violation. A larger cohort needed
  a separately frozen stage and remaining capacity; no diagnostic flag by itself
  authorized expansion. [Source](../../configs/maniguard/continuation/README.md)

- The earlier conditional planning envelope of as many as 12 valid episodes plus two
  infrastructure replacements did not become the executed allocation. The final
  screen freeze narrowed the immediate study to four starts and made later work
  conditional on the progress gate. [Source](../../docs/research/research_notes/ManiGuard%20continuation%20handoff%20contract.md); [Source](../../configs/maniguard/continuation/README.md)

- A progress-sensitive objective was subsequently implemented and checked only as
  prospective software. It preserves primary integer utility ordering on complete,
  equal-size panels and uses ordered progress only as a bounded secondary term, set
  to zero after any whole-episode violation. It was not integrated into the completed
  optimizer, actor or screen, and its physical benefit remains untested.
  [Source](../../configs/maniguard/continuation/progress-fitness-prototype-20261020.json)

### Inferences

- The protocol discipline is strong: the original comparison was completed despite
  poor intermediate results, while the later continuation idea was isolated behind
  its own source freeze, information labels, limits and advancement gate. This avoids
  retroactively improving the original pilot with exposed evidence.

- The recent work revised future plans, not completed-study facts. The main revision
  was a narrowing from a possible staged continuation study to a four-start screen,
  followed by a decision not to expand it. The proposed progress objective is now a
  candidate for a future protocol rather than a reinterpretation of past scores.

### Gaps

- No new protocol has yet frozen the controller representation, persistence rule,
  progress objective, ordinary-learning arm, cases, budgets and independent
  evaluation needed for the next scientific study.

- The repository does not contain physical evidence for the progress objective, a
  learned nonprivileged handoff trigger, a gripper-capable or persistent revised
  controller, or a matched ordinary-learning comparison.

## Completed execution, preparation, preservation and blockers

### Takeaway

Execution and preservation are complete for both recent physical studies. The result
is negative but usable: the native-noise integration worked, yet no condition safely
completed Jar, and the hard continuation handoffs did not produce qualifying progress.
The current blocker is the absence of a justified, funded next protocol with a more
competent control transition, not an unfinished queue or an unresolved evidence
restoration task.

### Cited Findings

- The original frozen pilot completed 60 of 60 full episodes, including all 16
  protected checking episodes, with zero invalid full starts. Every condition had
  0/4 requested safe completions and 0/4 requested unsafe completions. Native and EA
  each had one safe unfinished and three unsafe unfinished episodes, while fixed and
  random each had two safe unfinished and two unsafe unfinished episodes. Native and
  EA each reached one raw benchmark goal that failed the requested sequence.
  [Source](../../docs/research/reports/ManiGuard%20latent%20evolution%20pilot.md)

- All 40 comparison-training episodes were requested-unfinished; 16 violated. Four
  episodes achieved safe supported lid closure, but none extended that evidence to
  native holding. This means the completed search exposed partial progress without
  establishing safe completion or evolutionary advantage.
  [Source](../../configs/maniguard/latent-evolution/fitness-information-audit-20261020.json)

- The continuation screen completed all four 2,000-control trials with no invalid
  starts or replacements. Native-only and closure-only were safe unfinished;
  clock-handoff and privileged-handoff were unsafe unfinished. No trial achieved
  requested completion, raw completion or native holding, and neither handoff met
  the frozen progress rule. The clock trial violated before switching; the privileged
  trial violated after switching, but the evidence explicitly does not establish a
  unique action-specific cause. [Source](../../configs/maniguard/continuation/screen-summary-20261020.json);
  [Source](../../configs/maniguard/continuation/independent-screen-assessment-20261020.json)

- Admission controls were fully implemented before the screen ran. They required the
  complete original 60-episode result, independent design verification, restored
  evidence for all 60 episodes, preserved failed and partial starts, fresh resource
  and cutoff checks, sufficient whole-screen capacity and exclusive service ownership.
  The incomplete 42-episode proof was explicitly barred from opening admission.
  [Source](../../configs/maniguard/continuation/README.md)

- The final screen source freeze bound 34 execution and validation files to commit
  `38b3f50`, while the original comparison's 13 scientific files remained unchanged.
  An independent integration review then exercised the same frozen bytes with 21
  focused CPU cases and 15 copied-source probes before physical dispatch.
  [Source](../../configs/maniguard/continuation/source-freeze-v1-20261020.json);
  [Source](../../configs/maniguard/continuation/independent-integration-review-20261020.json)

- Physical evidence was independently restored and replayed for all 60 original
  episodes and all four screen episodes, including 112,095 and 8,004 exact native
  monitor entries respectively. The final portable catalog embeds 90 verified
  receipts, and its materialization record confirms byte-for-byte reconstruction.
  [Source](../../configs/maniguard/latent-evolution/original-60-physical-recovery-proof.json);
  [Source](../../configs/maniguard/continuation/screen-four-physical-recovery-20261020.json);
  [Source](../../configs/maniguard/latent-evolution/preservation-catalog-final-materialization-20261020.json)

- The final private round trip restored and checked 639 indexed analysis and paper
  artifacts. The completed record includes the 10-page paper, its bound inputs,
  figures, logs and independent reviews. No approval or credential blocker remained
  at that handoff. [Source](../../configs/maniguard/continuation/final-paper-analysis-recovery-20261020.json);
  [Source](../../docs/research/research_notes/ManiGuard%20execution%20replies.md)

- Final accounting records 70 physical starts in scope: 60 original full episodes,
  four screen episodes and six earlier engineering starts. It records 120,131 policy
  controls, 15,019 physical policy requests and 368 decoder-only calls. All authorized
  original and screen physical work was exhausted, and no physical worker was live at
  the final handoff. [Source](../../configs/maniguard/latent-evolution/pilot-closeout-20261020.json)

- The final allocation snapshot was not a provider-stop or billing confirmation. It
  recorded 45.4608 allocated GPU-hours and a USD 13.9245 rate-based upper estimate,
  while the historical instance was still marked running and the 20:00 UTC local
  watchdog remained armed. [Source](../../configs/maniguard/continuation/final-allocation-handoff-20261020.json)

- A fresh execution host still needs the pinned private archives, explicit Python
  environments, exact ManiGuard and OpenPI revisions, BEHAVIOR and robot assets,
  the CLIP asset and host-specific NVIDIA/EGL validation. Licensed bundles and their
  key are deliberately absent from backups. [Source](../../configs/maniguard/latent-evolution/README.md)

### Inferences

- There is no evidentiary reason to resume either completed queue. A resume command
  would be scientifically wrong because the budgets are exhausted and the screen's
  advancement gate failed.

- The integration was sufficiently functional to execute, account for, restore and
  replay the intended interventions. The negative outcome is therefore not simply an
  unexecuted method. It still does not prove that native-noise steering is generally
  ineffective, because this was one Jar pilot, one optimizer run and four checking
  blocks, with no ordinary-learning arm or cross-family transfer test.

- The next run is blocked first by scientific authorization: a new prospective
  protocol and budget are mandatory. Once those exist, execution is also contingent
  on reconstructing and validating the separately managed simulator, policy and
  licensed-asset environment on the chosen host.

### Gaps

- The final record does not verify the provider's eventual stop, final billed amount,
  stopped-storage charges or present availability of instance `56777435`.

- Two early Jar development episodes retain planned GPU mapping rather than the
  per-process GPU UUID receipts introduced before the comparative stages. This is a
  preserved provenance limitation, not a missing final-checking receipt.
  [Source](../../configs/maniguard/latent-evolution/design-verification-original60-20261020.json)

- Unknown physics before instrumentation callbacks remains unknown and must not be
  reported as zero. [Source](../../docs/research/reports/ManiGuard%20latent%20evolution%20pilot.md)

## Alignment assessment and most defensible next step

### Takeaway

Recent work remains aligned with the current direction as a completed native-noise
pilot plus a tightly bounded diagnosis. It would cease to be aligned if the
privileged hard-handoff screen were treated as the new main method, if its monitor
were described as an action-changing filter, or if another physical run began before
a new matched protocol and budget were frozen.

### Cited Findings

- The current direction asks whether a compact shared controller can recover useful
  behavior from a wholly frozen VLA using direct simulator outcomes. It requires an
  explanation of behavioral authority and an advantage over fixed settings, random
  search and ordinary learning. It also states that native-noise steering's physical
  benefit, evolutionary advantage and transfer are hypotheses rather than established
  results. [Source](../../Evolutionary%20improvement%20on%20ManiGuard.md)

- The completed actor matches much of that intended architecture: a 4 by 44 shared
  controller conditions on robot state, executed-action history and frozen visual and
  instruction features, modifies native flow noise, excludes family/task/monitor
  identity, keeps the VLA frozen and preserves proposed versus executed action
  records. [Source](../../docs/research/reports/ManiGuard%20latent%20evolution%20pilot.md)

- The study does not yet satisfy the full contribution test. It found no safe
  completion, no EA advantage and no transfer evidence. Its `ParameterReinforce`
  ordinary-learning interface has only CPU checks and no funded physical arm, and it
  does not substitute for a competent state-conditioned latent-learning baseline.
  [Source](../../docs/research/reports/ManiGuard%20latent%20evolution%20pilot.md)

- The continuation work stayed within the repository's safety interpretation rules:
  it reported completion, violation and unfinished categories together; kept the
  monitor passive; excluded the failed world model; charged all starts; and refused
  to infer irreversibility or causation from timeouts and event order.
  [Source](../../configs/maniguard/continuation/independent-screen-assessment-20261020.json);
  [Source](../../AGENTS.md)

- The current direction gives the latent extensions a clear order: begin with a small
  noise-scale and persistence diagnostic, use response-informed coordinates only if
  useful controllability is established, and leave internal activation gates as a
  separate later avenue. Every added decoder probe or physical trial needs explicit
  accounting and a prospective budget. [Source](../../Evolutionary%20improvement%20on%20ManiGuard.md)

- The closeout names the remaining scientific requirements as safe approach/grasp
  authority and stability, a newly frozen objective/controller and bounded budgets,
  then competent matched random or local search, ordinary learning, optimizer
  replication and frozen-controller transfer. It explicitly records zero remaining
  planned physical work under the completed protocols.
  [Source](../../configs/maniguard/latent-evolution/pilot-closeout-20261020.json)

### Inferences

- The most defensible next step is protocol design, not execution. Freeze a small
  development-only native-noise study that tests a shared gripper-capable controller
  and bounded persistence, uses the already prepared progress-sensitive objective,
  and declares its saved-input decoder budget, physical budget, validity rules and
  stop gates before any new call.

- The first gate should establish useful decoded control authority and a stable
  approach-to-grasp or closure-to-holding transition on development evidence. A fair
  grid or fixed-setting control is essential because noise scale and persistence are
  simple knobs. Response-informed coordinates should be admitted only after the
  bounded authority diagnostic succeeds.

- If that development gate passes, the subsequent physical comparison should retain
  native and tuned fixed controls, matched random search and a physically implemented
  ordinary-learning arm with the same observations, output domain and total
  interaction allowance. Fresh checking and cross-family transfer must stay reserved.
  If the gate fails, the predeclared bounded direct-residual fallback is more
  defensible than enlarging the failed hard-handoff continuation cohort.

- The continuation screen is still useful as diagnosis: it shows that preserving lid
  closure alone and switching back to native did not yield holding, lift, transport
  or safe completion on the exposed case. It should inform the next authority and
  fitness design, but it should not replace the main shared native-noise research bet.

### Gaps

- A new study must still choose and freeze the exact gripper/noise coordinates,
  persistence semantics, development families, fixed baselines, ordinary-learning
  algorithm, task and seed partitions, episode and query ceilings, resource window
  and advancement criteria.

- No current evidence estimates the violation rate, optimizer variance or transfer
  performance. With only four checking episodes per condition and no safe
  completions, the pilot supports a negative exploratory conclusion, not a general
  claim about ManiGuard or frozen-VLA adaptation.

- The current record cannot decide whether the failure is mainly missing task-policy
  competence, insufficient controller authority, unstable transition behavior or an
  uninformative sparse objective. The next protocol should be designed to separate
  these explanations rather than merely increasing search volume.
