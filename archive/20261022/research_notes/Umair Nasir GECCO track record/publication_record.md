# Umair Nasir GECCO publication and participation record

## Which GECCO publications, participation, awards, and service can be verified?

### Takeaway

Five GECCO proceedings contributions are verifiable as of 20 October 2026: three full
papers in the main proceedings and two poster papers in companion proceedings. Nasir is
first author on three of the five, including the 2026 paper *Mortar*, which was nominated
for the Evolutionary Machine Learning track's Best Paper Award. I found no reliable
evidence of a GECCO tutorial, workshop-organising role, program-committee role, conference
award win, or other GECCO service appointment.

### Inferences

- The verified record is publication-led, not service-led: the evidence supports authorship
  and a best-paper nomination, but not organising, reviewing, program-committee, workshop,
  or tutorial roles. This is an inference from the official proceedings and program searches,
  not proof that unlisted reviewing activity never occurred. [GECCO 2026 official site](https://gecco-2026.sigevo.org/);
  [Nasir's portfolio](https://umair-nasir14.github.io/)
- The two companion contributions should not be described simply as full GECCO papers.
  GECCO's own accepted-poster pages say these contributions appear in the companion volume
  and are shown in poster sessions. [GECCO 2023 accepted posters](https://gecco-2023.sigevo.org/Accepted-Posters.html);
  [GECCO 2026 accepted posters](https://gecco-2026.sigevo.org/Accepted%2BPosters)

### Gaps

- I found no official GECCO 2026 Best Paper Awards page that establishes the eventual
  Evolutionary Machine Learning winner. Therefore the strongest supported wording for
  *Mortar* is "Best Paper Award nominee", not winner.
- Public program and portfolio searches did not establish whether Nasir performed anonymous
  reviewing. Anonymous peer review is normally not recoverable from proceedings records.
- No evidence was located for GECCO publications in 2022 or earlier, or in 2025. Absence from
  the records searched should be reported as "none found", not as proof of no attendance.

## What are the dates, venues, tracks, authorship roles, and identifiers?

## How strong and sustained is the track record without overstating it?

### Takeaway

This is a strong and clearly relevant emerging GECCO record for a PhD-stage researcher: five
proceedings items over three GECCO years, three full papers, three first-author items, and one
verified best-paper nomination. It is not yet a long, uninterrupted, or service-heavy GECCO
record: there is no verified 2025 item, no verified award win, and no verified organising or
program role.

  full papers. The full papers are *LLMatic* in 2024, and *Mortar* plus *ProFiT* in 2026;
  the companion posters are *Augmentative Topology Agents* in 2023 and *Continuous Program

  would be overstated if it implied a long-standing senior-community role, multiple awards,

- For Sachin's GECCO 2027 safe-manipulation direction, Nasir appears potentially valuable as

  [official 2026 track assignments](https://gecco-2026.sigevo.org/Accepted%2BPapers)

  contributions across 2023, 2024, and 2026, including three full papers, three first-author
