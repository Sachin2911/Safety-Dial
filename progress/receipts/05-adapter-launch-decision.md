# Task 5 scored-pilot admission decision

24 October 2026. Admit the frozen 36-episode native/fixed/adaptive comparison
once both engineering recordings pass the existing validator. Keep all controller
settings, tasks, seeds, ordering and interaction ceilings in plan v4 unchanged.
This decision is based on the complete task 4 diagnostic, before any scored task 5
episode, and does not use the engineering tasks as an efficacy comparison.

The gate passes narrowly for an exploratory contact-execution hypothesis. Task 4
completed and validated all 12 native recordings. In task 6 seed 301, contact at
step 152 and closure at 221 precede a substantial post-action discrepancy before
tipping at 266. The final 40-action window has mean arm RMS error 0.015068 rad and
maximum 0.079954 rad, versus pre-contact mean 0.000102 rad. No target in that
window exceeds the pinned asset's joint limits. This is a relevant execution
discrepancy, although the trace cannot distinguish a cause of tipping from contact
resistance or a shared cause. Stronger feedback might worsen the interaction.

The dominant counterevidence remains explicit. Five of six violations occur with
close post-action tracking in their corresponding windows. Both unsafe goal
reaches carry an open jar. Three safe unfinished episodes close the lid without
grasping or lifting, with close tracking after closure. Larger late errors often
include commands beyond physical joint limits, which additional outward feedback
does not repair. The source-backed clipping audit is post-hoc and infers internal
targets; it is not a runtime measurement. Task 4 does not establish a general
tracking defect, and command choice and continuation remain the broader hypothesis.

The frozen protocol permits withholding the comparison when tracking is not
implicated. The contact exception supplies a limited mechanistic reason to test
the already specified intervention. The modest comparison can establish whether
this preset is a useful lever on safe requested completion, or supply evidence to
decline further search. No numerical admission threshold was prespecified, so this
is a documented research judgment, not a confirmatory gate test. Do not present
admission as evidence of benefit, an APEX reproduction or an evolutionary result.

Compare all three arms on the full preselected six-task panel, including cases
that argue against the mechanism. Do not select only the exceptional task, retune
from task 4, pool hosts, alter the requested-sequence score or inspect held-out
tasks. Tracking remains relative to native targets. Safety, completion, engagement
and unfinished episodes remain jointly reported. The two engineering episodes are
excluded from scored results.
